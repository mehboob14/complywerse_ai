## What This Evidences

The **Surveillance Audit Evidence Pack (FM)** supports ISO 41001:2018 ** — Internal Audit** by providing objective evidence that the organisation’s Facilities Management System (FMS) has been audited at planned intervals and that audit results were recorded, reported, and followed up.

This artifact is **collected, not authored**. It must be assembled from the organisation’s own operational systems, audit records, document repositories, issue/action tracking tools, and management review materials. Do **not** create or rewrite evidence for the audit pack; export or capture existing records that prove the surveillance audit cycle occurred.

## How To Collect

1. Identify the surveillance audit period: [start date] to [end date].
2. Locate the approved **internal audit programme/schedule** covering the period.
3. Export the completed internal audit reports relevant to ISO 41001 and FM processes.
4. Collect auditor assignment records showing independence, competence, and audit scope.
5. Export audit checklists, sampling notes, interview notes, or completed audit working papers.
6. Export all nonconformities, observations, improvement actions, and corrective actions raised from the audit.
7. Collect evidence of action ownership, due dates, status, verification, and closure.
8. Capture records showing audit results were reported to relevant management.
9. Include management review inputs/outputs that reference audit results, trends, risks, or improvement actions.
10. Export supporting operational evidence sampled during the audit, such as work orders, maintenance records, supplier performance reviews, service level reports, risk registers, or compliance checks.
11. Package the files into a dated ZIP or controlled Drive folder named: 
 **[YYYY-MM-DD] ISO41001 Surveillance Audit Evidence Pack - FM**
12. Create an index file listing each item, source system, export date, and owner.

## What To Capture

The pack should include sufficient records to show audit planning, execution, reporting, and follow-up:

- Internal audit programme/schedule, including audit dates and FM scope
- Audit plan showing criteria, scope, locations, processes, and assigned auditors
- Auditor competence and independence evidence
- Completed audit reports for ISO 41001 / FMS processes
- Audit checklists, sampling records, interview notes, and reviewed evidence lists
- Register of findings: conformities, nonconformities, observations, and opportunities for improvement
- Corrective action records with root cause, action plan, owner, target date, status, and verification of effectiveness
- Management reporting evidence: meeting minutes, dashboards, packs, or emails
- Management review records referencing audit outputs and improvement decisions
- Evidence from FM operations sampled during the audit, including:
 - maintenance records
 - service requests/work orders
 - statutory/compliance inspections
 - supplier performance records
 - asset or occupancy records
 - customer/user satisfaction or complaint records
 - risk and opportunity registers
- Screenshots or exports showing system timestamps, record IDs, and approval status

## Retention & Storage

Store the evidence pack in [Controlled DMS / SharePoint / GRC Repository] with restricted access and version control.

Recommended retention:

- Keep for at least **one full certification cycle plus the current cycle**, or
- Follow the organisation’s records retention schedule, if longer.

The storage location should preserve:

- Original exported files where possible
- Export timestamps
- Folder/index structure
- Access permissions
- Evidence integrity
- Audit trail of changes

## Acceptance Criteria

An auditor will typically accept the pack as sufficient when it:

- Clearly covers the surveillance audit period and ISO 41001 FMS scope
- Shows internal audits were planned and performed in line with
- Includes objective records from source systems, not recreated summaries
- Demonstrates auditor competence and impartiality
- Contains audit findings and evidence of communication to management
- Shows corrective actions were tracked, owned, and verified for effectiveness
- Links findings to relevant FM processes, locations, services, or suppliers
- Includes enough sampled operational records to support audit conclusions
- Is organised, indexed, dated, and traceable to source systems
- Can be independently followed by the auditor without relying on verbal explanation
