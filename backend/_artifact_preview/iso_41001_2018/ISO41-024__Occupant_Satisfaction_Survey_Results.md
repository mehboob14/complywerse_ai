## Purpose

This report presents the results of the Occupant Satisfaction Survey conducted to support the organisation’s Facility Management System (FMS) monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation activities under ISO 41001:2018.

The purpose of this report is to:

- Capture the voice of building users regarding facility management services, workplace conditions and service outcomes.
- Evaluate whether facility management performance is meeting user needs, organisational objectives and applicable service level expectations.
- Identify trends, nonconformities, improvement opportunities and areas requiring corrective or preventive action.
- Provide evidence for management review, continual improvement and facility planning decisions.
- Support objective evaluation of occupant satisfaction as a key performance indicator of facility management effectiveness.

This report is intended to provide documented information demonstrating that the organisation has determined what needs to be monitored and measured, selected appropriate methods, analysed results, and evaluated facility management performance in accordance with ISO 41001:2018.

### Corrective Action Requirements

Where findings indicate failure to meet defined service requirements, legal requirements, internal FM standards or FMS objectives, the responsible owner shall determine whether a corrective action record is required under the organisation’s nonconformity and corrective action process.

Corrective action shall be considered mandatory where:

- A safety or emergency readiness issue is confirmed.
- A service level failure has occurred repeatedly or materially affected occupants.
- A legal, accessibility, health or compliance requirement may not have been met.
- A defined FM objective or KPI has not been achieved and root cause analysis is required.
- Survey results indicate persistent dissatisfaction across multiple reporting periods.
