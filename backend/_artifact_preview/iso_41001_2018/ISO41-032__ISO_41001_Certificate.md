## Attestation Statement

[Organisation Name] attests that it holds a valid ISO 41001:2018 certificate issued by [Certification Body Name], an accredited certification body, confirming that the Facility Management Management System within the certified scope has been assessed and found to conform to the requirements of ISO 41001:2018.

Based on the certificate issued by [Certification Body Name], [Organisation Name]’s Facility Management Management System has been independently assessed against ISO 41001:2018 requirements, including the establishment, implementation, maintenance, and continual improvement of a management system for effective and efficient facility management.

The certified Facility Management Management System addresses the ISO 41001:2018 requirements relevant to:

- Understanding the organisational context and the needs and expectations of interested parties for facility management.
- Defining the scope of the Facility Management Management System.
- Leadership commitment, facility management policy, roles, responsibilities, and authorities.
- Planning for risks, opportunities, objectives, and actions necessary to achieve facility management outcomes.
- Support processes including resources, competence, awareness, communication, and documented information.
- Operational planning and control of facility management services within the certified scope.
- Performance evaluation, including monitoring, measurement, analysis, evaluation, internal audit, and management review.
- Continual improvement, including corrective action and improvement of the Facility Management Management System.

This Attestation is based on the current certificate and supporting certification records available as of the Effective Date stated above. Continued certification is subject to ongoing conformance with ISO 41001:2018, completion of required surveillance audits, closure of any certification body findings within required timeframes, and successful recertification before certificate expiry.

This Attestation does not replace the official certificate issued by [Certification Body Name]. In the event of any discrepancy between this Attestation and the official certificate, the official certificate and certification body records take precedence.
