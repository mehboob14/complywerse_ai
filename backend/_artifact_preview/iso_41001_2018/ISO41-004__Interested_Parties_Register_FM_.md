### Column Guidance

| Column | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Interested Party ID | Enter a unique register reference for the interested party, using a consistent format such as IP-FM-001. |
| Interested Party Category | Select the category of interested party. Allowed values: Occupant, Owner, Regulator, Contractor, Supplier, Service Provider, Employee, Visitor, Community, Emergency Service, Insurer, Other. |
| Interested Party Name | Enter the legal name, business name, department, group, or representative name of the interested party. |
| Relationship to Facility / FM System | Describe how the party interacts with or is affected by the facility management system, e.g. building occupant, landlord, statutory authority, maintenance contractor. |
| Primary Contact / Representative | Enter the named contact, role, email, and/or phone number where applicable; use 'TBC' if not yet confirmed. |
| Facility / Site / Service Scope Affected | Identify the facility, site, building, contract, service line, or FM system scope area relevant to this party. |
| Needs and Expectations | Record the interested party's broad needs and expectations relevant to facility management, such as safe workplace, statutory compliance, uptime, accessibility, service responsiveness, security, sustainability, or cost control. |
| Requirement Type | Classify the requirement. Allowed values: Legal, Regulatory, Contractual, Statutory, Standard, Policy, Operational, Financial, Health and Safety, Environmental, Accessibility, Security, Other. |
| Specific Requirement / Obligation | State the specific requirement relevant to the FM system in clear auditable language, including measurable criteria where available. |
| Source of Requirement | Enter the source document, law, regulation, lease, contract, SLA, permit, policy, stakeholder meeting, complaint, survey, or other source from which the requirement is derived. |
| Legal / Regulatory Applicability | Indicate whether the requirement is a legal or regulatory compliance obligation. Allowed values: Yes, No, To be assessed. |
| FM Objective / Process Impacted | Identify the FM objective, process, service, or procedure affected, such as planned maintenance, helpdesk, cleaning, security, space management, contractor management, emergency preparedness, compliance monitoring. |
| Risk or Opportunity if Requirement Is Not Met | Describe the consequence or opportunity associated with the need or requirement, such as non-compliance, occupant dissatisfaction, safety incident, service disruption, reputational damage, cost saving, performance improvement. |
| Priority / Significance | Assign significance based on the organisation's criteria. Allowed values: High, Medium, Low. Use High for legal/regulatory, safety-critical, business-critical, or contract-critical requirements unless otherwise justified. |
| Internal Owner | Enter the role accountable for monitoring and addressing the party's relevant requirements, e.g. FM Manager, Compliance Manager, Property Manager, HSE Manager, Contract Manager. |
| Current Method of Engagement / Communication | Describe how the organisation obtains, confirms, or communicates needs and requirements, e.g. contract review, tenant meeting, regulator notice, service review, survey, helpdesk, audit, inspection. |
| Engagement Frequency | Enter the planned frequency for engagement or monitoring. Allowed examples: Continuous, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, On contract review, On regulatory change, As required. |
| Evidence / Records Maintained | List the records retained to demonstrate understanding, monitoring, and fulfilment of requirements, e.g. meeting minutes, contracts, permits, inspection reports, service reports, survey results, compliance register. |
| Status of Understanding and Fulfilment | Record current status. Allowed values: Identified, Assessed, Requirements confirmed, Action required, Fulfilled, Under review, Not applicable. |
| Review Date | Enter the date this interested party entry was last reviewed in YYYY-MM-DD format. |
| Next Review Due | Enter the next scheduled review date in YYYY-MM-DD format, based on risk, significance, contractual cycle, or management review cycle. |
| Changes Since Last Review | Summarise any changes to the interested party, needs, expectations, requirements, contact details, applicability, or FM impact since the last review; enter 'None' if no change. |
| Related Document / Control Reference | Enter linked document or control references, such as ISO 41001 Cl. 4.2, compliance obligations register, contract ID, lease reference, SLA, procedure ID, risk register ID, or management review item. |

### Maintenance

The FM Manager owns this register with support from Compliance, HSE, Property, Procurement, and Contract Management. It is reviewed at least quarterly, and additionally after regulatory changes, contract changes, stakeholder feedback, incidents, complaints, or management review inputs, drawing from contracts, leases, compliance registers, helpdesk systems, stakeholder communications, surveys, audits, and regulatory updates.
