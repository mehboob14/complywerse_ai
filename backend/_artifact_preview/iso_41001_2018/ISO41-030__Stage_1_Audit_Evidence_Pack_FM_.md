## What This Evidences

The **Stage 1 Audit Evidence Pack (FM)** evidences that the organisation has planned, maintained, and retained appropriate documented information to support an ISO 41001:2018 Stage 1 audit, with specific relevance to ** Internal Audit**.

It demonstrates that the Facility Management System (FMS) has audit arrangements in place, including audit planning, scope, criteria, records, findings, and follow-up status. This pack is **collected from operational systems and repositories**; it is **not authored as a standalone document** and must not be fabricated.

The intent is to provide a structured ZIP file or secure shared-drive folder containing existing FMS documentation and records for auditor review.

## Where To Collect It From

Collect evidence from the organisation’s live FM and governance systems, typically including:

- Document management system: [DMS / SharePoint / Google Drive / Confluence]
- Internal audit management tool: [Audit Tool / GRC Platform]
- Facilities management platform / CAFM system: [CAFM / IWMS / CMMS]
- Corrective action or ticketing system: [ITSM / Action Tracker / Jira / ServiceNow]
- Meeting records repository: [Teams / SharePoint / Board Portal]
- Risk and compliance register: [Risk Register / GRC Tool]
- Training or competency system, if audit competence is in scope: [LMS / HRIS]

## How To Collect

1. Confirm the Stage 1 audit scope, sites, FM services, and audit period with the FMS owner or management representative.
2. Create a controlled collection folder named: `ISO41001_Stage1_EvidencePack_FM_[YYYY-MM-DD]`.
3. Export or download current approved FMS documented information from [DMS], including policy, scope, objectives, process descriptions, and audit procedure.
4. From [Audit Tool / GRC Platform], export the internal audit programme, latest internal audit plan, completed audit reports, findings, nonconformities, and follow-up status.
5. From [CAFM / IWMS / CMMS], export relevant FM operational records that support audited processes, such as maintenance schedules, work order samples, service performance dashboards, and statutory compliance logs.
6. From [Risk Register / GRC Tool], export FM risks, opportunities, controls, treatment actions, and review dates.
7. From [ITSM / Action Tracker], export corrective actions linked to internal audit findings, including owners, due dates, status, and closure evidence.
8. Include relevant management review inputs or outputs showing consideration of audit results.
9. Save exports in PDF, XLSX, CSV, or screenshot format, preserving system-generated metadata where available.
10. Compress the folder into a ZIP file or store it in a read-only shared-drive location.
11. Record a simple index listing each evidence item, source system, extraction date, and collector.

## What To Capture

The pack should include, where applicable:

- FMS scope and boundaries
- FM policy and objectives
- Internal audit programme covering ISO 41001 requirements
- Internal audit plan showing scope, criteria, auditor, date, and area audited
- Completed internal audit reports
- Audit findings, nonconformities, observations, and opportunities for improvement
- Corrective action records with owner, due date, status, and closure evidence
- Evidence of auditor competence or independence
- Management review records referencing audit results
- FM process evidence from [CAFM / IWMS / CMMS], such as work orders, maintenance compliance, service requests, and KPI reports
- Risk register extracts relevant to FM operations
- Document approval status, version, owner, and review date
- System timestamps and export dates

## Frequency & Owner

- **Frequency:** Before each Stage 1 audit, surveillance audit, recertification audit, or material FMS scope change.
- **Primary Owner:** [FMS Manager / Facilities Director]
- **Supporting Owners:** [Internal Audit Manager], [Compliance Manager], [Document Controller], [CAFM Administrator]
- **Collector:** [Assigned Evidence Coordinator]

## Retention & Storage

Store the ZIP or drive folder in [Controlled Evidence Repository] with read-only access for auditors and authorised personnel.

Minimum retention should align with the organisation’s documented information retention schedule, typically:

- Audit evidence packs: [3–6 years]
- Internal audit records: [3–6 years]
- Corrective action records: until closure plus [3 years]
- Statutory FM compliance records: as required by applicable legal and contractual obligations

Apply access control, version control, and deletion restrictions. Do not overwrite prior audit packs.

## Acceptance Criteria

An auditor will generally accept the evidence pack where it:

- Is clearly sourced from live organisational systems, not recreated manually
- Covers the defined FMS scope and audit period
- Demonstrates an internal audit programme and completed audit activity in line with ISO 41001:2018
- Includes audit criteria, scope, auditors, dates, results, and follow-up actions
- Shows corrective actions are tracked to closure or actively managed
- Contains approved and current FMS documentation
- Includes sufficient operational FM records to support the documented system
- Preserves source metadata such as export date, system name, record owner, and version
- Is indexed, complete, readable, and access-controlled
- Does not contain unexplained gaps, unverifiable screenshots, or manually fabricated records
