### Column Guidance

| Column | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Asset ID | Enter the unique internal identifier for the asset; use a consistent format such as AST-000001 and do not reuse retired IDs. |
| Asset Tag / Barcode | Enter the physical tag, QR code, barcode, or RFID reference attached to the asset; enter Not tagged if no tag exists. |
| Asset Name | Enter the commonly used asset name, such as Chiller 1, Fire Alarm Panel, Lift 2, or Generator A. |
| Asset Description | Enter a brief description sufficient to identify the asset, including capacity or key characteristics where relevant. |
| Asset Category | Select the broad category of asset; allowed values: Building Fabric, Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing, Fire & Life Safety, Security, ICT / AV, Furniture & Fixtures, Grounds / External, Fleet / Mobile Equipment, Other. |
| Asset Type | Enter the specific asset type, such as AHU, boiler, UPS, access control panel, roof membrane, pump, lift, desk, vehicle, or other defined type. |
| Location / Site | Enter the site or facility where the asset is located using the organisation's approved site name or code. |
| Building / Floor / Room / Area | Enter the precise physical location using building, floor, room, zone, plantroom, or area reference. |
| Functional Area Served | Enter the business, operational, or facilities area supported by the asset, such as Data Centre, Reception, Production Hall, Office Floor 3, or Whole Building. |
| Asset Owner | Enter the role or department accountable for the asset and lifecycle decisions, not an individual unless required by procedure. |
| Responsible FM Team / Custodian | Enter the team or contractor responsible for day-to-day control, operation, maintenance coordination, or inspection of the asset. |
| Manufacturer | Enter the manufacturer or brand name exactly as shown on the asset nameplate or documentation. |
| Model | Enter the manufacturer's model number or product reference; enter Not available if unknown. |
| Serial Number | Enter the manufacturer's serial number; enter Not available if not present. |
| Quantity | Enter the number of identical units represented by this register line; normally 1 for uniquely managed assets. |
| Unit of Measure | Enter the unit of measure for the quantity; allowed values: Each, Set, Metre, Square metre, Cubic metre, Lot, Other. |
| Acquisition Date | Enter the date the asset was purchased or obtained in YYYY-MM-DD format; leave blank only if not known. |
| Commissioning Date | Enter the date the asset was commissioned, placed into service, or accepted for operational use in YYYY-MM-DD format. |
| Purchase Order / Contract Reference | Enter the purchase order, contract, project, or capital expenditure reference that evidences acquisition or installation. |
| Supplier / Installer | Enter the supplier, installer, contractor, or integrator responsible for supplying or installing the asset. |
| Original Cost | Enter the original acquisition or installed cost excluding tax where possible; numeric value only, no currency symbol. |
| Currency | Enter the three-letter ISO currency code for cost values, such as USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, or ZAR. |
| Replacement Value | Enter the current estimated replacement cost for lifecycle planning; numeric value only, no currency symbol. |
| Expected Useful Life Years | Enter the expected useful service life in whole years based on manufacturer guidance, asset policy, or lifecycle plan. |
| Lifecycle Stage | Select the current lifecycle stage; allowed values: Planned, Acquired, Installed, Commissioned, In Service, Under Maintenance, Refurbishment, Renewal Planned, Decommissioned, Disposed. |
| Current Condition Rating | Select the latest condition rating; allowed values: 1-Excellent, 2-Good, 3-Fair, 4-Poor, 5-Failed / Unserviceable, Not assessed. |
| Condition Assessment Date | Enter the date the current condition rating was assessed in YYYY-MM-DD format. |
| Criticality Rating | Select the asset criticality rating based on the organisation's criticality method; allowed values: Critical, High, Medium, Low. |
| Criticality Rationale | Enter the reason for the criticality rating, considering safety, statutory compliance, service delivery, business continuity, customer impact, environmental impact, or cost impact. |
| Operational Impact if Failed | Describe the expected impact if the asset fails, including affected services, downtime, safety impact, compliance impact, or operational disruption. |
| Statutory / Regulatory Requirement | Enter Yes or No to indicate whether the asset is subject to legal, statutory, regulatory, licence, or mandatory inspection requirements. |
| Applicable Compliance / Inspection Requirement | If statutory or compliance requirements apply, enter the regulation, code, inspection type, certificate, or compliance obligation; otherwise enter Not applicable. |
| Warranty Expiry Date | Enter the warranty end date in YYYY-MM-DD format; enter Not applicable if no warranty applies. |
| Maintenance Strategy | Select the approved maintenance approach; allowed values: Reactive, Preventive, Predictive, Condition-Based, Statutory Inspection, Run-to-Failure, Outsourced Specialist, Other. |
| Preventive Maintenance Frequency | Enter the planned maintenance interval; use a clear format such as Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, 6-Monthly, Annual, 5-Yearly, Condition-based, or Not applicable. |
| Last Maintenance Date | Enter the date of the most recent completed maintenance, service, inspection, or test in YYYY-MM-DD format. |
| Next Maintenance Due Date | Enter the next planned maintenance, inspection, service, or test due date in YYYY-MM-DD format. |
| Maintenance Procedure / SOP Reference | Enter the approved maintenance instruction, SOP, method statement, task plan, statutory inspection checklist, or work order template reference. |
| Asset Documentation Location | Enter the controlled location or system link for manuals, drawings, certificates, warranties, O&M files, test records, or as-built documentation. |
| Energy / Utility Dependency | Enter key utilities or services required for the asset to operate; allowed examples: Electricity, Gas, Water, Steam, Compressed Air, Network, Fuel, None, Other. |
| Safety / Environmental Risk | Select the inherent safety or environmental risk associated with asset failure or operation; allowed values: High, Medium, Low, None identified. |
| Business Continuity Dependency | Enter Yes or No to indicate whether the asset supports a business continuity, emergency response, resilience, or mission-critical process. |
| Spare Parts Required | Enter key critical spares, consumables, or replacement parts required to maintain service, or enter Not identified. |
| Obsolescence Risk | Select the risk that the asset will become unsupported, uneconomic, or technologically obsolete; allowed values: High, Medium, Low, Not assessed. |
| Planned Renewal / Replacement Date | Enter the planned renewal, major refurbishment, or replacement date in YYYY-MM-DD format based on lifecycle planning. |
| Disposal / Decommissioning Date | Enter the actual or planned decommissioning or disposal date in YYYY-MM-DD format; leave blank while the asset remains in service unless planned. |
| Asset Status | Select the current register status; allowed values: Active, Inactive, Out of Service, Awaiting Repair, Under Replacement, Decommissioned, Disposed, Missing. |
| Record Last Updated Date | Enter the date this register entry was last reviewed or updated in YYYY-MM-DD format. |
| Record Updated By | Enter the name, role, or user ID of the person who last updated or verified the register entry. |
| Comments / Notes | Enter any additional relevant information needed to support facilities management planning, auditability, or lifecycle decisions; keep concise and factual. |

### Maintenance

The Facilities Asset Manager owns this template, with updates made whenever assets are acquired, moved, modified, maintained, reclassified, renewed, decommissioned, or disposed, and with a formal review at least annually. The register should draw from the CAFM/IWMS or CMMS, finance fixed asset records, procurement records, O&M documentation, statutory inspection records, and site condition surveys.
