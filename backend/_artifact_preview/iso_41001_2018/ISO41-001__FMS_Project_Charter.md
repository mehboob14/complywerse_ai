## Purpose & Objectives

This FMS Project Charter formally authorises the implementation of a Facility Management System (FMS) aligned with ISO 41001:2018 and establishes the mandate, leadership commitment, governance, authority, and resources required to implement, operate, maintain, and continually improve the FMS.

The Charter supports ISO 41001:2018 by demonstrating that top management has approved the FMS initiative, accepts accountability for the effectiveness of the FMS, and commits to providing direction, resources, oversight, and integration of facility management requirements into organisational processes.

The objectives of this Charter are to:

- Establish formal executive sponsorship and organisational authority for the FMS implementation.
- Confirm top management commitment to ISO 41001:2018 requirements, including leadership, strategic alignment, resource provision, risk-based thinking, performance evaluation, and continual improvement.
- Define the scope, implementation approach, governance model, responsibilities, resources, milestones, and success metrics for the FMS project.
- Ensure facility management objectives are aligned with organisational strategy, stakeholder needs, compliance obligations, and the demand organisation’s core business objectives.
- Provide a structured implementation plan to achieve readiness for ISO 41001:2018 conformity assessment and, where applicable, certification.
- Enable transparent monitoring and reporting of FMS implementation progress to senior leadership.
- Ensure the FMS is established as an integrated management system rather than a standalone documentation exercise.
- Mandate participation by relevant functions, including facilities, procurement, finance, health and safety, human resources, information technology, legal, sustainability, business continuity, security, quality, and operational business units.

The intended outcomes of the FMS implementation are:

- A documented, implemented, and maintained FMS that conforms to ISO 41001:2018.
- Clear facility management governance, accountabilities, decision rights, and escalation paths.
- Facility management policies, objectives, processes, performance indicators, and controls that support organisational effectiveness.
- Defined mechanisms for understanding demand organisation needs and translating them into facility management requirements.
- Improved consistency, reliability, safety, sustainability, service quality, cost transparency, and user satisfaction across facility management services.
- Evidence that top management demonstrates leadership and commitment through active participation, resourcing, communication, and review.

## Scope

This Charter applies to the planning and implementation of the FMS for the organisation’s facility management activities within the defined FMS scope.

The initial FMS implementation scope includes:

| Scope Element | Included |
|---|---|
| Organisation | [Organisation Name] |
| Facilities / Sites | [Head Office], [Operational Sites], [Warehouses], [Regional Offices], and other facilities approved by the FMS Steering Committee |
| Facility Management Services | Hard services, soft services, maintenance management, workplace services, space management, cleaning, security coordination, utilities management, waste management, contractor management, service desk coordination, facility-related procurement, and facility performance monitoring |
| Internal Functions | Facilities Management, Procurement, Finance, Health and Safety, Security, Sustainability, Legal, Human Resources, Information Technology, Risk Management, Business Continuity, and relevant business units |
| External Parties | Facility service providers, maintenance contractors, landlords, managing agents, outsourced FM partners, consultants, regulators, certification bodies, and other interested parties |
| Management System Components | Context analysis, stakeholder needs, scope definition, leadership governance, FMS policy, objectives, risks and opportunities, operational planning and control, support processes, performance evaluation, internal audit, management review, corrective action, and continual improvement |
| Standard | ISO 41001:2018 |

The implementation will address the requirements of ISO 41001:2018 across the following management system areas:

- Organisational context and interested party requirements.
- Leadership commitment and governance.
- Facility management policy and objectives.
- Roles, responsibilities, authorities, and competence.
- Resource planning and provision.
- Risk and opportunity management.
- Operational planning and control of facility management services.
- Communication, documented information, and knowledge management.
- Performance monitoring, measurement, analysis, and evaluation.
- Internal audit and management review.
- Nonconformity, corrective action, and continual improvement.

The following are outside the initial implementation scope unless specifically approved by the FMS Steering Committee:

- Full ISO 41001 certification for facilities not listed in the approved FMS scope.
- Capital project delivery governance unrelated to ongoing facility management operations.
- Technical engineering redesign of facilities, except where required to meet FMS objectives or compliance obligations.
- Replacement of enterprise systems unless approved as a dependency or remediation action.
- Non-facility-related service management processes.
- Legal compliance determinations outside facility management obligations.

Scope changes require documented approval by the FMS Steering Committee and must include impact assessment for resources, timeline, risk, stakeholder commitments, and certification readiness.

## Approach / Phases

The FMS implementation will follow a structured, phased approach designed to establish leadership commitment, define the management system, implement controls, evaluate performance, and prepare for certification or formal conformity assessment.

### Phase 1 — Initiation and Leadership Mandate

The purpose of this phase is to establish executive authority, governance, and commitment in accordance with ISO 41001:2018.

Key activities:

- Approve this FMS Project Charter.
- Appoint the Executive Sponsor, FMS Project Manager, FMS Steering Committee, process owners, and working group members.
- Confirm project objectives, scope, budget, authority, escalation routes, and decision-making protocols.
- Communicate top management commitment to the FMS across relevant functions and facilities.
- Confirm alignment between FMS objectives and organisational strategy, business requirements, sustainability objectives, compliance obligations, risk appetite, and service expectations.
- Establish project reporting cadence and leadership oversight arrangements.
- Confirm requirements for use of internal or external ISO 41001 expertise.

Primary outputs:

- Approved FMS Project Charter.
- Confirmed FMS governance structure.
- Resource and budget authorisation.
- FMS project communication from top management.
- Project plan and stakeholder engagement plan.

### Phase 2 — Current-State Review and Gap Assessment

The purpose of this phase is to evaluate existing facility management arrangements against ISO 41001:2018 requirements and identify required actions.

Key activities:

- Review existing FM policies, procedures, contracts, service specifications, performance reports, risk registers, compliance registers, asset records, and service level agreements.
- Assess leadership involvement, governance effectiveness, accountability, and resource sufficiency.
- Identify internal and external interested parties and their facility management requirements.
- Review demand organisation needs, business objectives, service expectations, and facility-related risks.
- Conduct site-level and service-level gap assessments.
- Evaluate existing supplier management, operational controls, maintenance practices, workplace services, emergency arrangements, and reporting mechanisms.
- Identify legal, regulatory, contractual, safety, environmental, accessibility, and sustainability obligations relevant to the FMS.
- Establish a prioritised implementation action plan.

Primary outputs:

- ISO 41001 gap assessment report.
- Interested party and requirements register.
- FMS risk and opportunity register.
- Initial compliance obligations register.
- Current-state process inventory.
- Prioritised remediation and implementation plan.

### Phase 3 — FMS Design and Documentation

The purpose of this phase is to design the management system structure and create required documented information.

Key activities:

- Define and approve the FMS scope statement.
- Develop the FMS Policy for approval by top management.
- Establish measurable FMS objectives aligned with strategic and operational priorities.
- Define governance structures, roles, responsibilities, authorities, and interfaces.
- Define facility management processes and their interactions.
- Develop required plans, procedures, registers, and templates.
- Establish document control, record retention, version management, and approval processes.
- Define competence requirements for FMS roles and develop training materials.
- Develop communication requirements for internal and external stakeholders.
- Define performance indicators, monitoring methods, data owners, reporting frequency, and review mechanisms.

Primary outputs:

- Approved FMS scope.
- Approved FMS Policy.
- FMS objectives and implementation plans.
- FMS process architecture and process interaction map.
- Roles and responsibilities matrix.
- Documented procedures and operational controls.
- FMS documented information register.
- Competence and training matrix.
- FMS KPI framework and reporting template.

### Phase 4 — Implementation and Operational Integration

The purpose of this phase is to implement the designed FMS and integrate it into routine facility management operations.

Key activities:

- Communicate approved FMS policy, objectives, roles, and expectations.
- Train personnel and contractors according to defined competence requirements.
- Implement facility management processes across in-scope services and sites.
- Align supplier requirements, contract management practices, service reporting, and performance reviews with the FMS.
- Implement risk and opportunity controls.
- Establish operational planning and control processes for in-scope FM activities.
- Integrate FMS requirements into procurement, contractor management, maintenance planning, work order management, service desk processes, incident management, health and safety coordination, sustainability reporting, and business continuity interfaces.
- Begin routine monitoring of FMS KPIs and service performance.
- Capture evidence of implementation, including meeting minutes, training records, approved documents, service reports, audit trails, and corrective actions.

Primary outputs:

- Implemented FMS processes.
- Training and competence records.
- Operational control evidence.
- Supplier performance records.
- Updated risk and opportunity register.
- KPI reports and performance dashboards.
- Records demonstrating communication and leadership engagement.

### Phase 5 — Performance Evaluation and Internal Assurance

The purpose of this phase is to evaluate the effectiveness of the implemented FMS and identify corrective actions before certification or formal review.

Key activities:

- Conduct internal audits against ISO 41001:2018 and organisational FMS requirements.
- Evaluate compliance with legal, regulatory, contractual, and stakeholder requirements.
- Review performance against FMS objectives and KPIs.
- Assess effectiveness of operational controls, supplier performance, risk treatment, and documented information management.
- Record nonconformities, observations, and improvement opportunities.
- Assign corrective actions with owners, due dates, and verification criteria.
- Conduct management review with top management participation.
- Confirm resource adequacy and leadership commitment for ongoing FMS operation.

Primary outputs:

- Internal audit programme and audit reports.
- Nonconformity and corrective action register.
- Compliance evaluation results.
- Management review agenda, minutes, decisions, and actions.
- Updated FMS performance report.
- Certification or conformity readiness assessment.

### Phase 6 — Certification Readiness and Transition to Business-as-Usual

The purpose of this phase is to finalise implementation, support external assessment where applicable, and transition the FMS into ongoing governance.

Key activities:

- Close priority corrective actions and verify effectiveness.
- Conduct final readiness review.
- Prepare evidence pack for external auditor or independent reviewer.
- Support Stage 1 and Stage 2 certification audit activities where certification is pursued.
- Respond to external audit findings and corrective action requests.
- Confirm ongoing FMS governance calendar, reporting cycle, internal audit programme, and management review schedule.
- Transfer ownership of FMS maintenance activities to business-as-usual process owners.
- Confirm continual improvement pipeline and resource planning.

Primary outputs:

- Certification readiness confirmation.
- External audit evidence pack.
- Certification audit reports, where applicable.
- Corrective action responses.
- Business-as-usual FMS governance calendar.
- Continual improvement register.
- Lessons learned report.

## Review & Update Triggers

This Charter will be reviewed at planned intervals and whenever significant changes affect the FMS implementation mandate, leadership commitment, resources, scope, or ability to meet ISO 41001:2018 requirements.

The Charter must be reviewed:

- At least at each major project phase gate.
- At least quarterly during active implementation.
- Prior to certification readiness review or formal conformity assessment.
- During management review where project governance, scope, resources, risks, or leadership commitments require amendment.
- Upon transition from project implementation to business-as-usual FMS governance.

The Charter must be updated when any of the following triggers occur:

| Trigger | Required Review Focus | Approval Required |
|---|---|---|
| Change in top management, Executive Sponsor, or FMS Project Manager | Confirm leadership commitment, authority, accountability, and continuity | Top Management / Executive Sponsor |
| Change to FMS scope, facilities, services, or organisational boundaries | Assess impact on deliverables, timeline, resources, risks, and certification readiness | FMS Steering Committee / Top Management |
| Material change to facility management strategy or organisational objectives | Reconfirm alignment of FMS objectives with strategic direction | Top Management |
| Significant budget increase or resource constraint | Reassess feasibility, schedule, scope, and prioritisation | Executive Sponsor / Finance Lead |
| Major supplier, outsourcing, or contract change affecting FM services | Update dependencies, operational controls, supplier requirements, and responsibilities | Procurement Lead / Steering Committee |
| New or changed legal, regulatory, contractual, safety, environmental, or stakeholder requirements | Update compliance obligations, controls, objectives, and implementation plan | Legal / Compliance Lead |
| Significant facility incident, service failure, audit finding, or compliance breach | Reassess risks, controls, resources, and management oversight | FMS Steering Committee |
| Internal audit identifies systemic FMS governance or implementation weaknesses | Update approach, milestones, responsibilities, or resources | Executive Sponsor |
| Certification body feedback or external audit findings affect implementation priorities | Update corrective action plan, timeline, and readiness criteria | FMS Project Manager / Steering Committee |
| Merger, acquisition, divestment, relocation, or major portfolio change | Reassess FMS scope, context, stakeholders, resources, and implementation plan | Top Management |
| System replacement or major change to FM, document control, reporting, or risk systems | Update dependencies, evidence collection, controls, and responsibilities | IT / Systems Lead |
| Transition to business-as-usual governance | Replace project milestones with ongoing FMS governance, audit, review, and improvement cycles | Facilities Management Lead / Executive Sponsor |

All updates to this Charter must be version controlled and approved in accordance with the organisation’s document control requirements. Superseded versions must be retained as documented information to evidence the evolution of leadership commitment, mandate, and resource decisions throughout FMS implementation.
