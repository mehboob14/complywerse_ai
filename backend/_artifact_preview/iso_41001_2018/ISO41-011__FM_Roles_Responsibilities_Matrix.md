### Column Guidance

| Column | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Matrix ID | Enter a unique identifier for the matrix line item, using a consistent format such as FM-RACI-001. |
| FM Activity / Decision | Enter the specific FM activity, responsibility, or decision being assigned, such as maintenance planning, contractor approval, space allocation, emergency response, or service performance review. |
| FM Process / Service Area | Enter the relevant FM process or service area, such as Asset Maintenance, Cleaning, Security, Catering, Space Management, Utilities, HSE, Procurement, Helpdesk, or Business Continuity. |
| ISO 41001:2018 Reference | Enter the relevant ISO 41001:2018 clause reference, normally Cl. 5.3; add related clauses where applicable, such as 6.1, 7.2, 7.5, 8.1, 9.1, or 10.2. |
| Activity Objective / Expected Output | Describe the intended outcome or deliverable of the activity in concise terms, such as approved maintenance plan, completed service review, authorised work order, or communicated FM responsibility. |
| Responsible Role(s) - R | Enter the role title(s) that perform or coordinate the work; use role titles, not personal names, and separate multiple roles with semicolons. |
| Accountable Role - A | Enter the single role title with ultimate accountability for ensuring the activity is completed and effective; only one Accountable role should be listed per row. |
| Consulted Role(s) - C | Enter role title(s) whose input must be obtained before or during the activity; use 'None' if not applicable and separate multiple roles with semicolons. |
| Informed Role(s) - I | Enter role title(s) that must be kept informed of status, outcome, or decisions; use 'None' if not applicable and separate multiple roles with semicolons. |
| Authority Granted | Describe the authority assigned to the Accountable or Responsible role, such as approve service schedules, assign FM resources, stop unsafe work, approve contractor access, or escalate nonconformities. |
| Decision / Approval Limit | Enter any financial, operational, risk, or scope limit for the authority, using measurable thresholds where applicable, such as up to USD 10,000, routine changes only, high-risk work excluded, or no financial authority. |
| Interfaces / Dependencies | Enter internal or external functions, suppliers, systems, or processes that interact with this activity, such as Procurement, HR, IT, Finance, HSE, outsourced FM provider, CAFM, CMMS, or landlord. |
| Required Competence or Qualification | Enter the required competence, training, licence, experience, or certification for the role performing the activity; use 'Defined in competence matrix' if controlled elsewhere. |
| Key Documented Information / Record | Enter the policy, procedure, form, system record, report, or evidence generated or used by the activity, such as work order, SLA report, contractor permit, asset register, inspection checklist, or meeting minutes. |
| Escalation Route | Enter the role or governance forum to which issues, conflicts, risks, or authority exceedances are escalated, such as Head of FM, FM Steering Committee, HSE Manager, Top Management, or Client Representative. |
| Frequency / Trigger | Enter when the activity occurs, using values such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, Per Work Order, Per Incident, Per Contract Renewal, On Change, or As Required. |
| Effective Date | Enter the date this RACI assignment becomes valid, in YYYY-MM-DD format. |
| Review Date | Enter the next scheduled review date for this assignment, in YYYY-MM-DD format. |
| Matrix Owner | Enter the role responsible for maintaining this matrix, typically Head of Facilities Management, FM Director, or FM Governance Manager. |
| Approval Status | Enter one allowed value: Draft, Under Review, Approved, Superseded, or Retired. |
| Approved By | Enter the role title or governance body that approved the assignment; use 'Pending approval' if not yet approved. |
| Approval Date | Enter the approval date in YYYY-MM-DD format; leave blank only where Approval Status is Draft or Under Review. |
| Change Notes | Briefly describe what changed since the previous version or enter 'Initial issue' for a new assignment. |

### Maintenance

This template is owned by the Head of Facilities Management or FM Governance Manager and is reviewed at least annually, and whenever there are organisational, contractual, service scope, process, or authority changes. It draws from the organisation chart, FM policy, job descriptions, competence matrix, FM procedures, service contracts, CAFM/CMMS, helpdesk system, and approval authority register.
