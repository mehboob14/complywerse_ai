### Column Guidance

| Column | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Service ID | Enter a unique catalogue identifier for the FM service, using a controlled format such as FMS-0001; do not reuse IDs. |
| Service Name | Enter the formal name of the FM service as it should appear in the approved service catalogue. |
| FM Service Category | Enter the service category, for example Hard FM, Soft FM, Workplace, Security, Cleaning, Catering, Grounds, Waste, Helpdesk, Energy, Space Management, or Other. |
| Service Description | Enter a concise description of what the service delivers and its intended outcome. |
| Business Need / Demand Requirement | Enter the identified organisational need, user requirement, demand driver, or operational requirement that justifies the service. |
| Service Customer / Recipient | Enter the internal or external customer group receiving the service, such as All Staff, Tenants, Visitors, Manufacturing, Data Centre Operations, or Named Department. |
| Site(s) / Asset(s) Covered | Enter the buildings, locations, zones, systems, or assets covered by the service; use site codes or asset register IDs where available. |
| Scope Included | Enter the specific activities, assets, tasks, service boundaries, and deliverables included in the service. |
| Scope Excluded | Enter activities, assets, tasks, or responsibilities explicitly excluded from the service to prevent ambiguity. |
| Service Owner | Enter the accountable internal role or named position responsible for defining and controlling the service. |
| Service Delivery Provider | Enter the internal team, contractor, supplier, or managing agent delivering the service. |
| Provider Type | Enter one allowed value: Internal, Outsourced, Hybrid, Landlord Provided, or Tenant Provided. |
| Contract / Agreement Reference | Enter the contract, purchase order, memorandum of understanding, internal service agreement, or supplier agreement reference; enter N/A only where no agreement applies. |
| SLA Reference | Enter the approved SLA, OLA, or service specification reference that defines agreed service levels; every active service should have an SLA reference. |
| Service Availability / Operating Hours | Enter the days and times the service is available, using clear format such as Mon-Fri 08:00-18:00, 24/7, or Business Hours excluding public holidays. |
| Request / Access Channel | Enter how users request or access the service, such as CAFM portal, helpdesk phone, email, planned schedule, BMS alert, or walk-up desk. |
| Priority Levels Applicable | Enter the priority categories used for this service, such as P1 Critical, P2 High, P3 Medium, P4 Low; align to the organisation's helpdesk or CAFM priority model. |
| Response Time SLA | Enter the agreed maximum time to acknowledge, attend, or respond by priority, including units, for example P1 15 min, P2 1 hr, P3 4 hrs. |
| Resolution / Completion Time SLA | Enter the agreed maximum time to restore, resolve, or complete by priority or task type, including units, for example P1 4 hrs, P2 1 business day. |
| Performance KPI(s) | Enter measurable performance indicators for the service, including target values and units, for example 95% jobs completed within SLA, <3 complaints/month. |
| Service Quality / Acceptance Criteria | Enter the defined standard for accepting the service as satisfactorily delivered, such as inspection pass score, statutory certificate issued, user sign-off, or no repeat fault within 7 days. |
| Planned Frequency / Schedule | Enter the planned delivery frequency or schedule, such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, On Demand, Reactive Only, or attach schedule reference. |
| Statutory / Regulatory Requirements | Enter applicable legal, regulatory, code, licence, permit, or compliance obligations for the service; include regulation name or reference where known. |
| ISO 41001 Process / Procedure Reference | Enter the internal FM procedure, operational control, method statement, work instruction, or process reference used to control delivery of this service. |
| Required Resources / Competencies | Enter required people, skills, certifications, tools, equipment, materials, or authorisations needed to deliver the service safely and effectively. |
| Criticality | Enter one allowed value: Critical, High, Medium, or Low, based on impact to business operations, safety, compliance, service users, and asset performance. |
| Continuity / Contingency Requirement | Enter the fallback, resilience, standby, emergency, or business continuity requirement for the service, including maximum tolerable disruption where applicable. |
| Key Risks / Operational Controls | Enter the main operational risks and the controls used to manage them, such as permit to work, RAMS, statutory inspection, access control, supervision, or supplier assurance. |
| Interfaces / Dependencies | Enter other services, systems, departments, suppliers, utilities, assets, or processes this service depends on or affects. |
| Monitoring Method | Enter how performance and conformity are monitored, such as CAFM reports, inspections, audits, BMS alarms, customer surveys, supplier reports, or statutory certificates. |
| Reporting Frequency | Enter how often performance is reported, such as Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, Per Event, or On Request. |
| Escalation Route | Enter the escalation path for missed SLAs, incidents, nonconformities, or service failures, including roles or teams rather than personal names where possible. |
| Change Control Requirement | Enter how changes to scope, SLA, provider, schedule, or service level are approved, such as FM Change Request, Contract Variation, Management Approval, or CAB. |
| Current Status | Enter one allowed value: Draft, Active, Suspended, Under Review, Retired. |
| Last Review Date | Enter the date this service catalogue entry was last formally reviewed, in YYYY-MM-DD format. |
| Next Review Due Date | Enter the next scheduled review date, in YYYY-MM-DD format. |
| Record Owner | Enter the role responsible for maintaining this catalogue entry, such as FM Service Catalogue Manager, Facilities Manager, Contract Manager, or Workplace Services Manager. |

### Maintenance

The FM Service Catalogue Manager owns this template, with service owners responsible for keeping their entries current; it should be reviewed at least annually and whenever a service, SLA, provider, site, statutory requirement, or operating model changes. Source data should be drawn from the CAFM/IWMS platform, approved SLAs and contracts, asset register, statutory compliance register, and FM operating procedures.
