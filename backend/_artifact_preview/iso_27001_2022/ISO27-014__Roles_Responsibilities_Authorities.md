## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to define and communicate the roles, responsibilities, and authorities within the Information Security Management System (ISMS) as per the requirements of ISO/IEC 27001:2022. This ensures that all personnel understand their specific obligations in maintaining and enhancing information security within the organization.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers who have access to [Organization]'s information assets and are involved in the ISMS. It covers all processes, systems, and data within the scope of the ISMS.

## Policy Statements

1. All roles and responsibilities related to the ISMS shall be clearly defined, documented, and communicated to relevant stakeholders.
2. The [Information Security Manager] shall have the authority to enforce information security policies and procedures across the organization.
3. All employees shall be made aware of their specific information security responsibilities and shall receive appropriate training to fulfill these responsibilities.
4. The ISMS roles and responsibilities shall be reviewed and updated at least annually or when significant changes occur in the organization or its environment.
5. The [Executive Management] shall ensure adequate resources are allocated to maintain the effectiveness of the ISMS.

## Roles & Responsibilities

| Role                        | Responsibilities                                                                 | Authority                                                                 |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Executive Management        | Approve ISMS policies, allocate resources, and ensure alignment with business objectives. | Final decision-making authority on ISMS matters.                           |
| Information Security Manager| Develop, implement, and maintain the ISMS, conduct risk assessments, and report on ISMS performance. | Authority to enforce ISMS policies and procedures.                         |
| IT Department               | Implement technical controls, monitor systems, and support ISMS initiatives.     | Authority to make technical decisions impacting information security.      |
| HR Department               | Conduct security awareness training and manage personnel security.                | Authority to manage employee compliance with ISMS policies.                |

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through regular audits and reviews of the ISMS. Non-compliance shall be addressed through corrective actions, which may include additional training, process changes, or disciplinary measures. The [Information Security Manager] is responsible for ensuring compliance and reporting any significant issues to [Executive Management].

## Exceptions

Any exceptions to this policy must be formally requested and approved by the [Information Security Manager] and documented with a clear rationale and a defined period for the exception. Exceptions shall be reviewed regularly to determine if they are still required.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually or when significant changes occur in the organization or its environment. The [Information Security Manager] is responsible for ensuring that the policy remains relevant and effective. Any changes to this policy must be approved by [Executive Management].
