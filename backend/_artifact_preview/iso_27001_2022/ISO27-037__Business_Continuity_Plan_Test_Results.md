## Purpose & Objectives

The purpose of this Business Continuity Plan (BCP) is to ensure that [Organization Name] can effectively respond to incidents and continue operations with minimal disruption. The objectives include establishing a structured approach to business continuity and disaster recovery (BC/DR), ensuring compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 control A.5.30, and documenting results from BC/DR exercises to enhance resilience.

## Scope

This plan applies to all critical business functions, processes, and IT systems within [Organization Name] that are essential for maintaining operations during and after a disruption. It encompasses all departments and locations, focusing on the continuity of services that directly impact [Organization Name]'s ability to meet its strategic objectives.

## Assumptions & Dependencies

- Critical business functions and IT systems have been identified and prioritized.
- Key personnel are available to execute the plan.
- External dependencies, such as third-party service providers, have their own BC/DR plans.
- Communication channels remain operational during a disruption.
- Regular updates to the plan are informed by changes in business operations, technology, and regulatory requirements.

## Approach / Phases

1. **Risk Assessment & Business Impact Analysis (BIA):** Identify potential risks and assess their impact on business operations.
2. **Strategy Development:** Develop strategies for maintaining and recovering critical business functions.
3. **Plan Development:** Document detailed procedures for BC/DR, including communication plans and recovery steps.
4. **Training & Awareness:** Conduct training sessions for staff on their roles and responsibilities within the BC/DR plan.
5. **Testing & Exercises:** Conduct regular tests and exercises to validate the effectiveness of the plan.
6. **Plan Maintenance:** Review and update the plan regularly to reflect changes in the organization and its environment.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **BCP Coordinator:** [Name] - Oversees the development and execution of the BC/DR plan.
- **IT Recovery Team:** Responsible for restoring IT systems and infrastructure.
- **Department Heads:** Ensure departmental compliance with the BC/DR plan and facilitate communication.
- **Communications Officer:** Manages internal and external communications during a disruption.

## Resources

- **Human Resources:** Trained personnel for executing the BC/DR plan.
- **Technical Resources:** Backup systems, data recovery tools, and communication devices.
- **Financial Resources:** Budget allocated for BC/DR activities, including training and testing.

## Success Metrics

- **Recovery Time Objective (RTO):** Achieve RTO for all critical functions as defined in the BIA.
- **Recovery Point Objective (RPO):** Meet RPO for critical data and systems.
- **Exercise Participation Rate:** Achieve 100% participation in scheduled exercises by key personnel.
- **Plan Update Frequency:** Ensure the plan is reviewed and updated at least annually or after significant changes.

## Review & Update Triggers

- **Annual Review:** Conduct a comprehensive review of the plan annually.
- **Post-Incident Review:** Update the plan following any significant incident or disruption.
- **Organizational Changes:** Revise the plan to reflect changes in organizational structure or business processes.
- **Regulatory Changes:** Update the plan in response to new or revised regulatory requirements.
