## Purpose

The purpose of this Information Classification & Handling Policy is to establish a framework for classifying and handling information assets in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022, specifically addressing control references A.5.12 and A.5.13. This policy ensures that information is appropriately protected based on its sensitivity, value, and criticality to the organization.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers who handle information assets owned, managed, or maintained by [Organization Name]. It covers all forms of information, including electronic, physical, and verbal communication, across all business units and locations.

## Policy Statements

1. **Information Classification Scheme**  
   1.1. Information assets shall be classified into the following categories: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted.  
   1.2. The classification of each information asset shall be determined by its sensitivity, value, and criticality to the organization.  
   1.3. All information assets shall be reviewed and classified by the asset owner at least annually or upon significant change.

2. **Handling Rules per Classification**  
   2.1. Public Information:  
       - May be freely shared without restrictions.  
       - Shall be reviewed periodically to ensure it remains appropriate for public disclosure.  
   2.2. Internal Information:  
       - Shall be accessible only to employees and authorized contractors.  
       - Shall be stored in systems with access controls to prevent unauthorized access.  
   2.3. Confidential Information:  
       - Shall be encrypted during transmission and storage.  
       - Access shall be restricted to individuals with a legitimate business need.  
   2.4. Restricted Information:  
       - Shall be subject to the highest level of security controls, including encryption and multi-factor authentication.  
       - Shall not be shared outside the organization without explicit authorization from senior management.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Information Asset Owners**: Responsible for classifying information assets and ensuring compliance with this policy.
- **Employees and Contractors**: Responsible for adhering to the handling rules specified for each classification level.
- **IT Security Team**: Responsible for implementing technical controls to protect information based on its classification.
- **Compliance Officer**: Responsible for monitoring adherence to this policy and conducting regular audits.

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through regular audits and assessments. Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract. The [Compliance Officer/Department] shall ensure that monitoring activities are conducted and reported to senior management.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted only under exceptional circumstances and must be approved by [Senior Management/Compliance Officer]. All exceptions shall be documented, including the rationale and duration of the exception.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed and updated at least annually or in response to significant changes in the organization or regulatory requirements. The [Policy Owner/Department] is responsible for ensuring the policy remains current and effective.
