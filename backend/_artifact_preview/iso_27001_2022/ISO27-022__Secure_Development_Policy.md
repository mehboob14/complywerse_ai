## Purpose

The purpose of this Secure Development Policy is to establish mandatory requirements for secure software development practices within [Organization Name]. This policy ensures compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 controls A.8.25 through A.8.32, focusing on secure software development lifecycle (SDLC), secure coding practices, management of test data, and oversight of supplier code.

## Scope

This policy applies to all software development activities conducted by [Organization Name], including those performed by third-party suppliers. It covers all phases of the SDLC, including design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance of software systems.

## Policy Statements

1. **Secure Development Lifecycle (SDLC):**  
   1.1. [Organization Name] shall implement a secure development lifecycle (SDLC) framework that integrates security best practices at each phase of software development.  
   1.2. Security requirements shall be defined, documented, and integrated into the project plan from the outset.

2. **Secure Coding Practices:**  
   2.1. Developers shall adhere to industry-recognized secure coding standards, such as OWASP or equivalent, to mitigate vulnerabilities.  
   2.2. Code reviews shall be conducted regularly to ensure compliance with secure coding standards.

3. **Management of Test Data:**  
   3.1. Test data shall be anonymized or de-identified to protect sensitive information during testing phases.  
   3.2. Access to test data shall be restricted to authorized personnel only.

4. **Supplier Code Management:**  
   4.1. All third-party code shall be assessed for security vulnerabilities and compliance with [Organization Name]'s secure coding standards before integration.  
   4.2. Contracts with suppliers shall include clauses that mandate adherence to secure development practices.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO):** Responsible for overall implementation and enforcement of this policy.
- **Development Team Leads:** Ensure compliance with secure coding practices within their teams.
- **Quality Assurance (QA) Manager:** Oversee the management of test data and ensure that security requirements are met during testing.
- **Procurement Manager:** Ensure that supplier contracts include necessary security clauses.

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through regular audits and code reviews. Any non-compliance shall be reported to the CISO and corrective actions shall be implemented promptly. Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

## Exceptions

Any exceptions to this policy must be documented and approved by the CISO. Exceptions shall be granted only where justified by a risk assessment and where compensating controls are in place.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed annually or upon significant changes to the development processes or regulatory requirements. The CISO is responsible for ensuring the policy remains current and effective.
