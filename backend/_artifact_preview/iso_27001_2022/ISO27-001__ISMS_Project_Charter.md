## Purpose & Objectives

The purpose of this ISMS Project Charter is to formally authorize the initiation and implementation of an Information Security Management System (ISMS) in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022. The primary objectives are to establish a structured framework for managing information security, ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements, protect information assets, and enhance stakeholder confidence.

## Scope

The scope of the ISMS includes all information assets, processes, and systems within [Organization Name] that are critical to business operations. This encompasses all departments, including IT, HR, Finance, and Operations, and applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors with access to the organization's information systems.

## Assumptions & Dependencies

- The project assumes full support from senior management and adequate resource allocation.
- Dependencies include the availability of existing IT infrastructure, cooperation from all departments, and alignment with ongoing business initiatives.
- Successful implementation is dependent on the timely completion of each project phase and the engagement of all stakeholders.

## Approach / Phases

The implementation of the ISMS will be conducted in the following phases:

1. **Initiation**: Establish project governance, define scope, and secure resources.
2. **Planning**: Conduct risk assessment, define ISMS policy, and develop risk treatment plan.
3. **Implementation**: Deploy controls, conduct training, and integrate ISMS into business processes.
4. **Monitoring & Review**: Perform internal audits, management reviews, and continuous improvement.
5. **Certification**: Engage with a certification body for external audit and certification.
