## Purpose
The purpose of this Document Control Procedure is to establish a standardized method for the creation, approval, distribution, version control, and retention of documents related to the Information Security Management System (ISMS) in compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022, specifically addressing control requirement Cl. 7.5.

## Scope
This procedure applies to all documents within the ISMS, including policies, procedures, guidelines, and records, ensuring they are adequately controlled to maintain the integrity and confidentiality of information.

## Prerequisites & Inputs
- Access to the Document Management System (DMS)
- Understanding of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements
- List of current ISMS documents
- Document Control Register

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Document Creation**
   - Draft the document using the standard template.
   - Ensure alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.
   - Submit the draft for initial review by the [Document Owner].

2. **Document Approval**
   - Forward the reviewed document to the [Approval Authority].
   - Obtain formal approval documented via electronic signature or approval form.
   - Record the approval in the Document Control Register.

3. **Document Distribution**
   - Distribute the approved document to relevant stakeholders via the DMS.
   - Ensure access permissions are set according to the document's classification.

4. **Version Control**
   - Assign a unique version number upon approval.
   - Update the version history in the document header and Document Control Register.
   - Archive previous versions in the DMS with restricted access.

5. **Document Retention**
   - Retain documents in accordance with the organization's retention policy.
   - Review and update documents at least annually or upon significant changes.
   - Dispose of documents securely when they are no longer required.

## Records & Outputs
- **Document Control Register**: A log of all documents including title, version, approval date, and owner.
  - Example:
    | Document Title          | Version | Approval Date | Owner         |
    |-------------------------|---------|---------------|---------------|
    | ISMS Policy             | 1.0     | [Approval Date] | [Document Owner] |

- **Version History**: Embedded in each document, detailing changes and approvals.

## Exceptions & Escalation
- Exceptions to this procedure must be documented and approved by the ISMS Manager.
- Escalate unresolved issues to the [Senior Management] for resolution.

## Review
This procedure shall be reviewed annually or when significant changes occur in the ISMS or related standards. The review will be conducted by the [Document Owner] and approved by the [Approval Authority].
