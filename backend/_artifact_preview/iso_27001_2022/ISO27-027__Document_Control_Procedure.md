## Purpose

This procedure defines how documented information required by the Information Security Management System (ISMS) is created, reviewed, approved, distributed, controlled, retained, protected, and disposed of in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

The purpose is to ensure that ISMS documentation is:

- Available and suitable for use where and when needed.
- Adequately protected from loss of confidentiality, improper use, or loss of integrity.
- Properly identified, described, reviewed, approved, version controlled, and retained.
- Withdrawn from use when obsolete.
- Managed consistently across the document lifecycle.

This procedure supports compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements for documented information, including creation and updating, control, availability, preservation, distribution, access, retrieval, use, storage, change control, retention, and disposition.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all documented information required by the ISMS or determined by the organisation as necessary for the effectiveness of the ISMS, including but not limited to:

- ISMS scope statement.
- Information security policy and supporting policies.
- Risk assessment and risk treatment methodology.
- Risk assessment records.
- Risk treatment plan.
- Statement of Applicability.
- Information security objectives.
- Procedures, standards, guidelines, and work instructions.
- Internal audit programme, plans, checklists, and reports.
- Management review inputs and outputs.
- Corrective action records.
- Evidence of competence, awareness, training, and communication.
- Records demonstrating operation of controls.
- Supplier security documentation relevant to the ISMS.
- Legal, regulatory, contractual, and compliance records relevant to information security.
- Other ISMS records retained as evidence of conformity and effective operation.

This procedure applies to documented information in all formats and media, including:

- Electronic documents.
- Records stored in governance, risk and compliance systems.
- Tickets, approvals, workflow records, and logs.
- Spreadsheets and registers.
- Signed PDFs.
- Email approvals where explicitly permitted.
- Paper records, where used.

This procedure applies to all personnel involved in creating, reviewing, approving, using, maintaining, or retiring ISMS documented information, including employees, contractors, and relevant third parties.

## Step-by-Step Procedure (numbered)

1. **Identify the need for documented information**

 1.1. The Document Owner, Information Security Manager, Compliance Manager, Internal Auditor, control owner, or process owner identifies the need to create, update, withdraw, or archive documented information.

 1.2. The need may arise from one or more of the following:

 - ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.
 - Evidence required to demonstrate ISMS conformity.
 - Operation of ISMS processes.
 - Implementation or operation of Annex A controls.
 - Audit findings or corrective actions.
 - Risk assessment or risk treatment activities.
 - Management review decisions.
 - Changes in organisational context, interested parties, or ISMS scope.
 - Legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations.
 - Periodic review cycle.

 1.3. The initiator records the request using the approved document change request method.

 | Field | Required Information |
 |---|---|
 | Request type | Create, revise, retire, archive, restore, or emergency update |
 | Document title | Name of the document or proposed document |
 | Business justification | Reason for the request |
 | ISO/IEC 27001:2022 relevance | Applicable clause, Annex A control, or ISMS process |
 | Requested by | Name or role of requester |
 | Requested date | Date request submitted |
 | Required completion date | Target completion date, if applicable |
 | Impacted stakeholders | Functions, teams, or third parties affected |
 | Classification | Proposed information classification |
 | Retention requirement | Proposed retention period or record type |

2. **Determine whether the document is controlled documented information**

 2.1. The ISMS Document Controller reviews the request and determines whether the item is controlled documented information under the ISMS.

 2.2. A document or record must be controlled where it:

 - Is required by ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
 - Is required by the organisation for effective ISMS operation.
 - Provides evidence of conformity.
 - Defines a mandatory or repeatable security process.
 - Supports information security risk management.
 - Supports legal, regulatory, contractual, or audit obligations.
 - Requires approval, version control, retention, or restricted access.

 2.3. If the item is not controlled documented information, the ISMS Document Controller records the decision and directs the requester to the appropriate unmanaged or operational storage location.

 2.4. If the item is controlled documented information, the procedure continues.

3. **Assign document metadata**

 3.1. The ISMS Document Controller assigns or confirms the required document metadata before drafting or revision.

 3.2. Each controlled ISMS document must include, at minimum:

 | Metadata Element | Requirement |
 |---|---|
 | Document ID | Unique identifier assigned according to the ISMS document naming convention |
 | Title | Clear and descriptive document title |
 | Version | Version number using the approved version numbering method |
 | Owner | Role accountable for maintaining the document |
 | Approver | Role authorised to approve the document |
 | Effective date | Date from which the document applies |
 | Review date or frequency | Next scheduled review or approved review interval |
 | Classification | Information classification label |
 | Framework reference | Relevant ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause or Annex A control reference |
 | Status | Draft, in review, approved, effective, superseded, archived, or withdrawn |
 | Retention period | Applicable retention rule |
 | Repository location | Controlled storage location |
 | Access group | Authorised users or groups |

 3.3. Controlled documents must use approved organisational templates where available.

 3.4. The document title, owner, classification, and control reference must be accurate and suitable for the document purpose.

4. **Create or revise the documented information**

 4.1. The Document Owner drafts or revises the document using the approved template and style appropriate to the artifact type.

 4.2. The content must be:

 - Accurate, complete, and suitable for its intended use.
 - Aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.
 - Consistent with approved ISMS policies and risk treatment decisions.
 - Written clearly enough for intended users to understand their responsibilities.
 - Free from obsolete references, unsupported claims, or conflicting requirements.
 - Classified according to the organisation’s information classification scheme.
 - Traceable to applicable ISMS requirements, controls, processes, or records.

 4.3. Where the document is a procedure, work instruction, checklist, or operational standard, the Document Owner must ensure it defines sufficient detail for consistent execution.

 4.4. Where the document is a record, form, register, or evidence artifact, the Document Owner must ensure it contains enough information to demonstrate conformity and effective ISMS operation.

 4.5. The Document Owner must avoid storing current controlled ISMS documentation in unmanaged locations, personal drives, local folders, or email inboxes.

5. **Apply version control**

 5.1. The ISMS Document Controller ensures that version control is applied before review and approval.

 5.2. The following versioning convention must be used unless otherwise approved:

 | Version Type | Format | Use |
 |---|---|---|
 | Initial draft | 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 | Draft versions before first approval |
 | First approved issue | 1.0 | First formally approved version |
 | Minor revision | 1.1, 1.2, 1.3 | Non-material updates that do not change requirements or responsibilities |
 | Major revision | 2.0, 3.0 | Material changes to scope, requirements, roles, process, controls, or obligations |
 | Emergency revision | Current version plus emergency notation in change record | Urgent change requiring accelerated approval |

 5.3. Each controlled document must include or be linked to a change history.

 | Version | Date | Description of Change | Author | Reviewer | Approver |
 |---|---|---|---|---|---|
 | 1.0 | [DATE] | Initial approved issue | [ROLE/NAME] | [ROLE/NAME] | [ROLE/NAME] |

 5.4. The ISMS Document Controller must ensure that superseded versions are removed from general use and retained only in the controlled archive.

 5.5. Obsolete documents must be clearly marked as superseded, archived, or withdrawn to prevent unintended use.

6. **Review the document**

 6.1. The Document Owner submits the draft or revised document for review.

 6.2. Reviewers must be selected based on relevance to the document content. Reviewers may include:

 - Information Security Manager.
 - Compliance Manager.
 - Risk Manager.
 - Control owner.
 - Process owner.
 - Legal representative.
 - Human Resources representative.
 - IT Operations representative.
 - Privacy or Data Protection representative.
 - Internal Audit representative.
 - Supplier or third-party management representative.

 6.3. Reviewers assess whether the document is:

 - Suitable for its intended purpose.
 - Aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
 - Consistent with ISMS scope, policies, objectives, risk assessment, and Statement of Applicability.
 - Operationally feasible.
 - Accurate and complete.
 - Appropriately classified.
 - Supported by necessary records or forms.
 - Free of conflicts with existing approved documentation.
 - Compliant with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.
 - Assigned an appropriate retention period.

 6.4. Reviewer comments must be recorded in the approved review workflow, repository comment function, or document change request record.

 6.5. The Document Owner resolves reviewer comments before submission for approval.

 6.6. Unresolved material comments must be escalated to the Information Security Manager.

7. **Approve the document prior to issue**

 7.1. Controlled ISMS documents must be reviewed and approved for suitability and adequacy before issue.

 7.2. The approver must have authority appropriate to the document type and impact.

 | Document Type | Minimum Approver |
 |---|---|
 | ISMS scope | Executive management or ISMS governance body |
 | Information security policy | Executive management or designated senior accountable owner |
 | ISMS procedures | Information Security Manager |
 | Risk assessment methodology | Information Security Manager and Risk Manager |
 | Risk treatment plan | Risk owner or executive management, as applicable |
 | Statement of Applicability | Information Security Manager and executive management or ISMS governance body |
 | Standards and work instructions | Relevant control owner and Information Security Manager |
 | ISMS records and forms | Document Owner or Information Security Manager |
 | Internal audit documentation | Internal Audit Lead |
 | Management review records | Chair of management review or executive sponsor |

 7.3. Approval may be evidenced through:

 - Electronic workflow approval.
 - Digital signature.
 - Repository approval status.
 - Meeting minutes recording formal approval.
 - Email approval retained as an ISMS record, where workflow approval is not available.

 7.4. Approval evidence must identify:

 - Document title and version.
 - Approver name or role.
 - Approval date.
 - Approval decision.
 - Any conditions of approval.

 7.5. Documents must not be published as approved or effective until approval evidence has been captured.

8. **Publish and distribute the approved document**

 8.1. The ISMS Document Controller publishes the approved version to the controlled ISMS repository.

 8.2. The ISMS Document Controller ensures that:

 - Only the current approved version is available for normal use.
 - Access permissions are consistent with classification and need-to-know.
 - The document is retrievable by authorised users.
 - The effective date is visible.
 - Superseded versions are removed from current access locations.
 - External distribution is controlled, where applicable.

 8.3. The Document Owner identifies affected users and stakeholders requiring notification, training, or acknowledgement.

 8.4. Distribution may be performed through:

 - Controlled repository notification.
 - ISMS communication channel.
 - Training or awareness platform.
 - Email distribution with link to the controlled document.
 - Governance or management meeting communication.
 - Supplier portal or contractual communication route, where applicable.

 8.5. Where distributed outside the organisation, the Document Owner must ensure that:

 - External distribution is authorised.
 - The classification permits external release.
 - Recipients are identified.
 - Distribution records are retained.
 - Any confidentiality, contractual, or non-disclosure requirements are met.
 - Uncontrolled copies are clearly marked unless controlled external access is provided.

9. **Control access, retrieval, and use**

 9.1. The Repository Administrator grants access to controlled documented information based on approved roles, classification, and need-to-know.

 9.2. Edit access must be restricted to authorised Document Owners, delegates, ISMS Document Controllers, and approved contributors.

 9.3. Approval rights must be restricted to authorised approvers.

 9.4. Read access must be sufficient to ensure that personnel can access information required to perform their ISMS responsibilities.

 9.5. The repository must support retrieval using document title, document ID, owner, classification, status, or control reference.

 9.6. Downloading, printing, or forwarding restricted documents must be controlled according to the applicable classification and acceptable use requirements.

 9.7. Users must refer to the controlled repository as the authoritative source. Copies downloaded, printed, or emailed are considered uncontrolled unless explicitly designated as controlled copies.

10. **Protect documented information**

 10.1. The ISMS Document Controller and Repository Administrator must ensure that documented information is protected from:

 - Unauthorised access.
 - Unauthorised modification.
 - Accidental deletion.
 - Loss of confidentiality.
 - Loss of integrity.
 - Loss of availability.
 - Improper use.
 - Obsolescence or unintended use.

 10.2. Protection measures must include, as applicable:

 | Protection Requirement | Control Measure |
 |---|---|
 | Confidentiality | Classification labels, role-based access, encryption where required |
 | Integrity | Version control, approval workflow, restricted edit permissions |
 | Availability | Central repository, backup, recovery capability, access monitoring |
 | Authenticity | Approval evidence, change history, metadata |
 | Preservation | Retention schedule, archival controls, format readability |
 | Prevention of unintended use | Superseded watermark, archive segregation, current-version-only publication |

 10.3. Repositories storing controlled ISMS documentation must be backed up or otherwise protected in accordance with approved backup and resilience requirements.

 10.4. Access to restricted ISMS documents must be reviewed periodically or upon role change, termination, or classification change.

11. **Maintain the document register**

 11.1. The ISMS Document Controller maintains a document register of controlled ISMS documented information.

 11.2. The register must include, at minimum:

 | Register Field | Description |
 |---|---|
 | Document ID | Unique identifier |
 | Document title | Name of document |
 | Artifact type | Policy, procedure, standard, record, plan, register, report, form, etc. |
 | Owner | Accountable owner |
 | Approver | Authorised approver |
 | Version | Current approved version |
 | Status | Draft, approved, effective, superseded, archived, withdrawn |
 | Effective date | Date document became effective |
 | Review frequency | Required review cycle |
 | Next review date | Scheduled review date |
 | Classification | Information classification |
 | Retention period | Required retention duration |
 | Repository location | Link or location |
 | ISO/IEC 27001:2022 reference | Clause, Annex A control, or ISMS process |
 | Last review date | Date of most recent review |
 | External distribution | Yes or no, and recipient group if applicable |

 11.3. The register must be updated when a controlled document is created, approved, revised, superseded, withdrawn, archived, restored, or disposed of.

12. **Perform periodic review**

 12.1. Each controlled ISMS document must be reviewed at the defined frequency or sooner when a triggering event occurs.

 12.2. Unless a stricter requirement applies, the following minimum review frequencies apply:

 | Document Category | Minimum Review Frequency |
 |---|---|
 | ISMS scope | Annually or upon material organisational change |
 | Information security policy | Annually |
 | Risk assessment methodology | Annually or upon material risk process change |
 | Risk assessment records | At planned risk assessment intervals or upon significant change |
 | Risk treatment plan | At least annually and after risk assessment updates |
 | Statement of Applicability | At least annually and after significant risk or control changes |
 | Procedures and standards | Annually |
 | Work instructions | Annually or upon process change |
 | Internal audit records | Per audit programme and retention schedule |
 | Management review records | Per management review schedule and retention schedule |
 | Training and awareness records | Per retention schedule |
 | Supplier security records | Per supplier review schedule and retention schedule |

 12.3. Triggering events requiring out-of-cycle review include:

 - Change to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements or certification scope.
 - Significant security incident.
 - Material change to risk assessment or risk treatment.
 - Major organisational restructuring.
 - Introduction of new systems, services, suppliers, or technologies.
 - Change in legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations.
 - Internal or external audit finding.
 - Corrective action requiring document change.
 - Management review decision.
 - Obsolete, inaccurate, or conflicting content identified.

 12.4. The Document Owner records the outcome of the review as one of:

 - No change required.
 - Minor update required.
 - Major revision required.
 - Document to be withdrawn.
 - Document to be replaced by another controlled document.

 12.5. Review evidence must be retained even when no changes are made.

13. **Handle document changes**

 13.1. Changes to controlled ISMS documents must follow the review and approval steps in this procedure.

 13.2. The Document Owner must assess whether the change affects:

 - ISMS scope.
 - Information security objectives.
 - Risk assessment or risk treatment.
 - Statement of Applicability.
 - Policies or mandatory requirements.
 - Roles and responsibilities.
 - Legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations.
 - Control design or operation.
 - Training or awareness requirements.
 - Supplier or customer commitments.

 13.3. Material changes must be communicated to affected stakeholders before or on the effective date.

 13.4. Where changes require user acknowledgement or training, the Document Owner must define and track completion.

 13.5. Change records must be retained with the document or in the approved workflow system.

14. **Control externally originated documented information**

 14.1. The Document Owner identifies externally originated documented information necessary for the planning and operation of the ISMS.

 14.2. Examples include:

 - Laws, regulations, and regulatory guidance.
 - Contractual security requirements.
 - Customer security obligations.
 - Supplier security documentation.
 - Standards, codes of practice, or certification requirements.
 - External audit reports.
 - Penetration test reports from third parties.
 - Threat intelligence or advisory documents used in ISMS decisions.

 14.3. Externally originated documented information must be identified, classified, and controlled where necessary to ensure its suitability and availability.

 14.4. The Document Owner must ensure that externally originated documents are:

 - Stored in an approved location.
 - Identified as externally originated.
 - Linked to relevant ISMS processes or obligations.
 - Protected according to classification and contractual requirements.
 - Reviewed for currency where applicable.
 - Distributed only to authorised recipients.

15. **Retain records**

 15.1. ISMS records must be retained for the period defined in the retention schedule.

 15.2. Retention periods must consider:

 - ISO/IEC 27001:2022 evidence requirements.
 - Certification audit cycles.
 - Legal and regulatory obligations.
 - Contractual commitments.
 - Business requirements.
 - Litigation hold or investigation requirements.
 - Risk and assurance needs.

 15.3. Unless otherwise required by law, contract, or approved retention schedule, the following minimum retention periods apply:

 | Record Type | Minimum Retention Period |
 |---|---|
 | Current approved ISMS policies and procedures | Retain while current |
 | Superseded ISMS policies and procedures | 3 years after supersession |
 | ISMS scope versions | 3 years after supersession |
 | Risk assessment records | 3 years after supersession or completion |
 | Risk treatment plans | 3 years after supersession or completion |
 | Statement of Applicability versions | 3 years after supersession |
 | Internal audit reports and evidence | 3 years |
 | Management review records | 3 years |
 | Corrective action records | 3 years after closure |
 | Training and awareness records | 3 years or duration of employment plus 1 year, whichever is longer |
 | Access review records for ISMS repositories | 3 years |
 | Document approval records | Life of document plus 3 years |
 | Document change request records | 3 years after closure |
 | External distribution records | 3 years after distribution or withdrawal |

 15.4. Records subject to legal hold, investigation, audit dispute, regulatory inquiry, or active corrective action must not be destroyed until the hold is formally released.

16. **Archive superseded or withdrawn documents**

 16.1. When a document is superseded, withdrawn, or no longer required for current use, the ISMS Document Controller moves it to the controlled archive.

 16.2. Archived documents must be:

 - Clearly marked as superseded, withdrawn, or archived.
 - Removed from current-use locations.
 - Retained for the applicable retention period.
 - Protected from unauthorised access, alteration, or deletion.
 - Retrievable for audit, legal, regulatory, or business purposes.
 - Disposed of only after retention requirements are met.

 16.3. The document register must be updated to reflect archive status, archive date, and retention end date.

17. **Dispose of documents and records**

 17.1. At the end of the retention period, the ISMS Document Controller identifies documents and records eligible for disposal.

 17.2. Before disposal, the ISMS Document Controller must confirm with the Document Owner and Compliance Manager that:

 - Retention period has expired.
 - No legal hold applies.
 - No open audit, corrective action, investigation, dispute, or regulatory matter requires continued retention.
 - Disposal is permitted by law, contract, and organisational retention requirements.

 17.3. Disposal must be performed securely according to the document format and classification.

 | Format | Disposal Method |
 |---|---|
 | Electronic records | Secure deletion or approved repository purge process |
 | Paper records | Cross-cut shredding or approved secure destruction service |
 | Removable media | Secure wiping, cryptographic erase, or physical destruction |
 | Cloud repository records | Approved deletion process with audit trail where available |
 | Backups | Expire through approved backup retention cycle unless specific purge is required and feasible |

 17.4. Disposal evidence must be retained and must include:

 - Description of disposed records.
 - Date of disposal.
 - Disposal method.
 - Person or service performing disposal.
 - Approval for disposal.
 - Confirmation of legal hold check.

18. **Monitor compliance with this procedure**

 18.1. The Information Security Manager monitors compliance with this procedure through periodic checks, internal audits, management review inputs, and document register review.

 18.2. Compliance checks may include:

 - Sampling documents for approval evidence.
 - Verifying document metadata completeness.
 - Confirming current versions are available at points of use.
 - Confirming obsolete versions are removed from normal use.
 - Checking review dates are current.
 - Reviewing access permissions.
 - Verifying retention and archival controls.
 - Confirming external distribution records.
 - Checking document register accuracy.

 18.3. Nonconformities must be recorded and managed through the corrective action process.

## Exceptions & Escalation

Exceptions to this procedure are permitted only where formally justified, risk assessed, approved, and recorded.

Examples of exceptions include:

- Emergency publication of a document before completion of the standard review cycle.
- Temporary use of an alternative repository due to service outage.
- Retention beyond the standard period due to legal hold or regulatory requirement.
- Restricted distribution of normally available ISMS documentation due to confidentiality concerns.
- Use of email approval where workflow approval is unavailable.
- Delayed periodic review due to dependency on an active investigation, audit, or regulatory change.

Exception requests must include:

| Field | Required Information |
|---|---|
| Exception requester | Name and role of requester |
| Document affected | Document ID, title, and version |
| Procedure requirement affected | Specific requirement for which exception is requested |
| Justification | Business or compliance reason |
| Risk assessment | Confidentiality, integrity, availability, legal, regulatory, audit, and operational impacts |
| Compensating controls | Measures to reduce risk during exception period |
| Duration | Start date and end date |
| Approver | Information Security Manager or higher authority where required |
| Review date | Date exception will be reviewed |
| Closure evidence | Evidence that exception ended or was converted to approved practice |

Escalation requirements:

| Scenario | Escalation Path | Required Action |
|---|---|---|
| Material disagreement on document content | Document Owner → Information Security Manager | Resolve or refer to ISMS governance body |
| Approval delay affecting ISMS compliance | ISMS Document Controller → Information Security Manager → Approver | Prioritise approval or define interim control |
| Suspected use of obsolete document | User or Document Owner → ISMS Document Controller | Remove obsolete document and notify affected users |
| Unauthorised change to controlled document | ISMS Document Controller → Information Security Manager → Incident Management process if applicable | Preserve evidence, restore approved version, investigate |
| Loss or corruption of controlled documentation | Repository Administrator → Information Security Manager | Restore from backup and assess impact |
| Unauthorised disclosure of restricted documented information | Any person → Information Security Manager | Assess as potential information security incident |
| Retention or disposal conflict | ISMS Document Controller → Compliance Manager → Legal, if required | Suspend disposal until resolved |
| Repeated noncompliance with this procedure | Information Security Manager → ISMS governance body or executive management | Initiate corrective action |

Emergency changes:

- Emergency document changes may be approved through an accelerated process when delay would create unacceptable information security, legal, regulatory, operational, or audit risk.
- Emergency approval must be obtained from the Information Security Manager or delegated authority.
- The emergency change must be retrospectively reviewed and fully documented within 10 business days.
- If the emergency change is not confirmed through normal approval, it must be withdrawn or replaced by an approved version.

## Review

This procedure must be reviewed at least annually and whenever one or more of the following occurs:

- Changes to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 or certification requirements affecting documented information.
- Material change to the ISMS scope.
- Change to the controlled document repository or approval workflow.
- Significant audit finding related to document control.
- Significant information security incident involving documentation integrity, availability, or confidentiality.
- Change to legal, regulatory, contractual, or retention requirements.
- Management review decision requiring update.
- Repeated exceptions or nonconformities related to document control.

The Information Security Manager is accountable for ensuring this procedure remains suitable, adequate, and effective. The ISMS Document Controller is responsible for coordinating the review and maintaining review evidence.

Review activities must include:

- Confirming alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
- Confirming that document control responsibilities remain accurate.
- Assessing whether document metadata, approval, versioning, retention, and distribution requirements remain effective.
- Reviewing exceptions, nonconformities, and audit results.
- Confirming the document register is complete and current.
- Confirming retention periods remain appropriate.
- Identifying improvement opportunities.

Review outcomes must be recorded as one of:

- Approved with no changes.
- Approved with minor changes.
- Major revision required.
- Procedure withdrawn or replaced.

The review record must include the reviewer, review date, outcome, summary of changes or rationale for no change, and approval evidence.
