## Purpose & Objectives

The purpose of this document is to outline the roadmap for implementing the Information Security Management System (ISMS) in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022. The primary objective is to establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve an ISMS that aligns with the strategic goals of [Organization Name] and complies with the requirements of the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 standard.

## Scope

This plan applies to all departments, business units, and personnel within [Organization Name] involved in the ISMS implementation. It encompasses all processes, information systems, and physical locations that handle or store information assets deemed critical to the organization’s operations.

## Assumptions & Dependencies

- Senior management will provide the necessary support and resources for ISMS implementation.
- All stakeholders will actively participate in the ISMS processes.
- External consultants may be engaged for specific expertise.
- Dependencies include the availability of current IT infrastructure, existing policies, and procedures, and alignment with other ongoing projects.

## Approach / Phases

1. **Initiation Phase**
   - Define ISMS scope and boundaries.
   - Conduct a preliminary risk assessment.
   - Secure management commitment.

2. **Planning Phase**
   - Develop ISMS policy and objectives.
   - Identify and assess risks.
   - Determine risk treatment options.

3. **Implementation Phase**
   - Implement risk treatment plans.
   - Develop and implement required controls.
   - Conduct awareness and training programs.

4. **Monitoring & Review Phase**
   - Conduct internal audits.
   - Review ISMS performance and effectiveness.
   - Implement corrective actions as necessary.

5. **Improvement Phase**
   - Conduct management reviews.
   - Update and improve ISMS based on audit findings and reviews.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **ISMS Program Manager**: Oversee the entire ISMS implementation process and ensure alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
- **Risk Management Team**: Conduct risk assessments and develop treatment plans.
- **IT Security Team**: Implement technical controls and manage IT-related risks.
- **Internal Audit Team**: Perform regular audits to assess ISMS effectiveness.
- **Senior Management**: Provide strategic direction and allocate resources.

## Resources

- Financial budget for ISMS-related activities.
- Access to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 standard documentation.
- External consultancy services for specialized expertise.
- Training programs for staff awareness and competence development.

## Success Metrics

- Completion of ISMS implementation within the defined timeline and budget.
- Reduction in the number and severity of information security incidents.
- Achieving ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification.
- Positive feedback from internal audits and management reviews.

## Review & Update Triggers

- Changes in organizational structure or business processes.
- Significant security incidents or breaches.
- Updates to the ISO/IEC 27001 standard.
- Feedback from audits or management reviews indicating necessary improvements.
