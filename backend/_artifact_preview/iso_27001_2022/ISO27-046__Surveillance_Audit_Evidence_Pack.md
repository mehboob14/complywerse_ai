## What This Evidences

The **Surveillance Audit Evidence Pack** supports ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ** – Internal Audit** by demonstrating that the organisation’s ISMS audit programme, audit execution, findings, corrective actions, and management visibility are operating effectively between certification / surveillance audit cycles.

This artifact is **collected, not authored**. Do not create or rewrite evidence for the audit. Compile exports, records, screenshots, reports, and system-generated evidence from the organisation’s actual operational systems.

## Where To Collect It From

Collect evidence from the systems used to plan, perform, track, and report ISMS audits and related corrective actions, typically:

- Audit management / GRC platform: `[GRC/Audit Tool Name]`
- ITSM or issue tracking system: `[ITSM/Ticketing Tool Name]`
- Document management system: `[DMS/SharePoint/Confluence/Google Drive Name]`
- Risk register / risk management platform: `[Risk Register Tool Name]`
- Management review repository: `[Board Portal/Meeting Records Location]`
- Internal audit working papers repository: `[Audit Evidence Repository]`
- Corrective action tracker: `[CAPA Tracker/System Name]`

## How To Collect

1. Create a secure folder named: 
 `[YYYY] ISO27001 Surveillance Audit Evidence Pack`

2. Export or download the approved **internal audit programme / schedule** covering the surveillance period from `[GRC/Audit Tool Name]`.

3. Collect completed internal audit records, including:
 - audit plans
 - audit scope
 - audit criteria
 - auditor assignment
 - audit dates
 - audit reports
 - sampled controls /. Export audit findings and nonconformities from `[GRC/Audit Tool Name]` or `[ITSM/Ticketing Tool Name]`.

5. Export corrective action records showing status, owners, due dates, remediation evidence, and closure approvals.

6. Collect evidence that audit results were reported to relevant management, such as management review minutes, audit committee packs, or ISMS steering group records.

7. Collect evidence of auditor independence and competence, such as auditor assignment records, training records, or auditor CV / certification records where maintained.

8. Include an index file listing each evidence item, source system, export date, and owner.

9. Compress the folder into a ZIP file or store it in the controlled audit evidence drive: 
 `[Secure Audit Evidence Location]`.

## What To Capture

The evidence pack should include, at minimum:

- Internal audit programme for the surveillance period
- Audit schedule showing planned vs completed audits
- Audit scope and criteria, including ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clauses and applicable ISMS boundaries
- Names and roles of auditors and auditees
- Evidence of auditor independence from audited areas
- Completed internal audit reports
- Audit findings, observations, nonconformities, and opportunities for improvement
- Corrective action records with:
 - finding reference
 - severity / classification
 - assigned owner
 - root cause, where applicable
 - agreed action
 - due date
 - status
 - closure evidence
 - closure approval
- Evidence of management review or leadership reporting
- Screenshots or exports showing system-generated timestamps and workflow status
- Export metadata showing source system and collection date

## Frequency & Owner

- **Frequency:** Annually, before the ISO 27001 surveillance audit, and updated if additional internal audits or corrective actions occur before the auditor review.
- **Primary Owner:** `[ISMS Manager / Compliance Manager]`
- **Supporting Owners:** `[Internal Audit Lead]`, `[Risk Owner]`, `[ITSM Owner]`, `[Document Control Owner]`

## Retention & Storage

Store the final ZIP or controlled drive folder in `[Secure Audit Evidence Location]` with restricted access for ISMS, audit, and authorised leadership personnel only.

Retain for at least:

- the current certification cycle, plus the previous cycle; or
- `[Organisation Retention Period]`, if longer; or
- any contractual, regulatory, or legal requirement that applies.

Ensure the evidence pack is protected against unauthorised alteration. Use version control, access logs, or read-only permissions where available.

## Acceptance Criteria

An auditor should accept the pack as sufficient where it shows that:

- an internal audit programme existed and was approved for the surveillance period;
- audits were performed against ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and the organisation’s ISMS scope;
- audit results were documented and traceable to audit criteria;
- auditors were competent and independent of the areas audited;
- findings and nonconformities were recorded, assigned, tracked, and closed or actively managed;
- corrective actions include evidence of remediation and approval where closed;
- audit results were communicated to relevant management;
- records are sourced from controlled systems and include dates, owners, and status;
- evidence is complete, current, and traceable to the organisation’s live ISMS processes.
