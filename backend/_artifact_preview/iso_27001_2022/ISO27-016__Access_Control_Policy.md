## Purpose

The purpose of this Access Control Policy is to establish the principles and requirements for managing logical and physical access to information systems and facilities within [Organization Name]. This policy ensures that access to information assets is controlled and managed in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022, specifically addressing control reference A.5.15.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party users who have access to [Organization Name]'s information systems and physical facilities. It covers all information assets, including hardware, software, and data, whether located on-premises or in the cloud.

## Policy Statements

1. Access to information systems and physical facilities shall be granted based on the principle of least privilege, ensuring that users have the minimum level of access necessary to perform their job functions.

2. All access requests shall be formally documented, approved by the appropriate authority, and reviewed periodically to ensure continued relevance and appropriateness.

3. User access rights shall be promptly modified or revoked upon changes in employment status, job role, or termination, to prevent unauthorized access to information assets.

4. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) shall be implemented for access to critical systems and sensitive information to enhance security controls.

5. Physical access to facilities housing critical information systems shall be controlled through the use of secure entry mechanisms and monitored by surveillance systems.

6. Access to information systems shall be logged and monitored to detect and respond to unauthorized access attempts or anomalies.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Information Security Manager**: Responsible for the overall implementation and management of the Access Control Policy, including periodic reviews and updates.

- **IT Department**: Responsible for the technical implementation of access controls, including the configuration of systems and monitoring access logs.

- **Human Resources**: Responsible for notifying the IT Department of any changes in employment status or job roles that may affect access rights.

- **Line Managers**: Responsible for initiating access requests for their team members and ensuring that access is appropriate and necessary for job functions.

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through regular audits and access reviews. Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract. Access logs shall be reviewed periodically to ensure adherence to this policy.

## Exceptions

Any exceptions to this policy must be formally documented and approved by the Information Security Manager. Exceptions shall be granted on a case-by-case basis and reviewed regularly to ensure they remain justified.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed annually or upon significant changes to the organization or its information systems. The Information Security Manager is responsible for coordinating the review process and ensuring that the policy remains aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.
