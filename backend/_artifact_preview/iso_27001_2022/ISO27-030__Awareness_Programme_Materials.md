## Purpose

The purpose of this template is to standardize the creation and maintenance of materials for the organization's Information Security Awareness Programme. This includes slide decks, posters, and phishing tests, aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 control A.6.3, which focuses on ensuring that employees are aware of information security threats and their responsibilities.

## Instructions

1. **Identify Audience and Objectives**: Determine the target audience for the awareness materials and define the specific objectives of the session or campaign.
2. **Develop Materials**: Create slide decks, posters, and phishing test scenarios that are engaging, informative, and aligned with the objectives. Ensure content is relevant to the audience's role and responsibilities.
3. **Review and Approval**: Submit materials for review and approval by the Information Security Officer or designated authority before dissemination.
4. **Distribution and Tracking**: Distribute materials through appropriate channels (e.g., email, intranet, physical posters) and track engagement and completion rates.
5. **Feedback and Improvement**: Collect feedback from participants to improve future awareness activities.

## Records & Retention

- **Record Keeping**: Maintain records of all awareness materials, including drafts, approvals, and feedback, in the Information Security Management System (ISMS) repository.
- **Retention Period**: Retain all records for a minimum of 3 years to comply with audit and compliance requirements.
- **Access Control**: Ensure records are accessible only to authorized personnel to maintain confidentiality and integrity.
