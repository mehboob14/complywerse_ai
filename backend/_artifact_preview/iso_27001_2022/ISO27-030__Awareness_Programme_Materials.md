## Purpose

This template is used to plan, document, approve, deliver, and retain evidence of information security awareness programme materials supporting ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A.6.3.

Annex A.6.3 requires personnel and relevant interested parties to receive appropriate information security awareness, education, and training, including updates to organisational policies, topic-specific guidance, and responsibilities relevant to their roles. This template provides a consistent format for creating and evidencing awareness materials such as:

- Slide decks and training modules
- Posters, intranet content, email campaigns, and newsletters
- Phishing simulation notices, test scenarios, and learning materials
- Role-based awareness content for privileged users, managers, developers, contractors, and other relevant groups
- Refresher communications following incidents, audit findings, risk assessments, or policy changes

The completed form supports audit evidence by showing that awareness content is planned, relevant, approved, delivered, measured, and retained.

## Instructions

1. Complete this template for each awareness material, campaign, training module, poster set, phishing simulation, or awareness communication package.
2. Use one form per distinct awareness item or campaign. For example:
 - One form for the annual mandatory security awareness training deck.
 - One form for a phishing simulation campaign.
 - One form for a poster campaign on reporting suspicious emails.
3. Ensure the material aligns to applicable information security policies, acceptable use rules, incident reporting expectations, data protection requirements, and role-specific security responsibilities.
4. Identify the intended audience clearly, including whether the material applies to:
 - All personnel
 - Employees only
 - Contractors or temporary workers
 - Privileged users
 - Developers or technical teams
 - Managers
 - Third-party users with access to organisational information or systems
5. Include measurable learning or awareness objectives where practical.
6. Obtain approval before publication, delivery, or launch, especially for phishing simulations and organisation-wide communications.
7. Ensure phishing tests are ethically designed, lawful, proportionate, and approved by relevant stakeholders before execution.
8. Attach or reference the actual awareness materials, such as slide decks, posters, emails, landing pages, LMS modules, intranet articles, screenshots, or phishing templates.
9. After delivery, update the completion and effectiveness fields with evidence such as attendance, completion rates, quiz results, phishing click rates, reporting rates, feedback, and remediation actions.
10. Retain the completed form and supporting evidence in the approved records repository.

## Records & Retention

Completed awareness programme material forms and associated evidence must be retained as controlled information security management system records.

Records should demonstrate that information security awareness, education, and training were planned, approved, delivered, and evaluated in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A.6.3.

| Record Type | Examples | Retention Requirement | Record Location | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Completed Awareness Programme Materials Form | This completed template, approval records, routing history | Minimum 3 years or longer if required by legal, regulatory, contractual, or audit obligations | [Records repository / GRC system] | [Record Owner] |
| Approved Awareness Content | Slide decks, LMS modules, posters, email copy, newsletters, intranet screenshots, videos, quick reference guides | Retain current version plus prior versions for minimum 3 years | [Content repository / LMS / intranet archive] | [Information Security / L&D Owner] |
| Training Delivery Evidence | Attendance registers, LMS completion reports, acknowledgements, webinar logs, onboarding completion records | Minimum 3 years | [LMS / HR system / records repository] | [HR / L&D Owner] |
| Assessment Evidence | Quiz results, pass/fail records, knowledge check summaries, completion certificates | Minimum 3 years | [LMS / records repository] | [HR / L&D Owner] |
| Phishing Simulation Evidence | Approved simulation plan, email templates, landing page screenshots, campaign metrics, results report, remediation records | Minimum 3 years, subject to privacy and employment law requirements | [Phishing platform / records repository] | [Security Operations / Awareness Owner] |
| Effectiveness and Improvement Records | Metrics dashboards, lessons learned, improvement actions, management reporting | Minimum 3 years | [GRC system / records repository] | [GRC Owner] |
| Review and Supersession Records | Review notes, version history, superseded materials, change approvals | Minimum 3 years after supersession | [Document management system] | [Document Owner] |

Retention and handling requirements:

- Store records in an approved repository with access restricted to authorised roles.
- Protect individual-level training, assessment, and phishing simulation results as confidential personnel-related information.
- Do not retain real passwords, unnecessary personal data, or excessive behavioural monitoring data.
- Ensure records are available for internal audit, external audit, management review, and certification assessment.
- Dispose of records securely after the approved retention period, subject to legal hold, investigation, regulatory, contractual, or audit requirements.
- Review awareness materials at least annually and whenever significant changes occur to threats, policies, technology, legal obligations, organisational context, or incident trends.
