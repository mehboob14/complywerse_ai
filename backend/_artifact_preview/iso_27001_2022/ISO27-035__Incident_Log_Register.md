## Purpose

The purpose of this Incident Log / Register is to systematically record all security events and incidents, along with the corresponding responses, to ensure compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 control objectives A.5.24 to A.5.28. This register serves as a critical tool for tracking, managing, and analyzing security incidents to enhance the organization's information security posture.

## Scope

This register applies to all security events and incidents occurring within [Organization Name], affecting any information assets, including hardware, software, and data. It encompasses incidents detected by automated systems, reported by personnel, or identified through other means.

## Maintenance Process & Cadence

The Incident Log / Register is maintained by the [Information Security Team/Department]. Entries must be recorded as incidents are identified and updated as they progress. The register is reviewed and updated at least monthly to ensure accuracy and completeness. Historical data should be archived annually for future reference and analysis.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Information Security Manager**: Oversees the incident management process, ensures timely updates to the register, and conducts monthly reviews.
- **Incident Response Team**: Responsible for investigating incidents, implementing response actions, and updating the register with relevant details.
- **IT Support Staff**: Assists in identifying incidents, executing technical response measures, and providing necessary information for the register.

## Review

This document and the Incident Log / Register are subject to an annual review to ensure alignment with organizational changes, emerging threats, and updates to the ISO/IEC 27001 standard. The review will be conducted by the [Information Security Manager] and approved by [Senior Management/Executive Sponsor].
