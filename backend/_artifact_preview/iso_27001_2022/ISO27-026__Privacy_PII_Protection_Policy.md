## Purpose

The purpose of this Privacy / PII Protection Policy is to establish mandatory requirements for the protection of Personally Identifiable Information (PII) in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022, specifically addressing control A.5.34. This policy aims to ensure that PII is handled and processed in a manner that preserves privacy and protects against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers who have access to, process, or manage PII within [Organization Name]. It encompasses all systems, processes, and data repositories where PII is stored, processed, or transmitted.

## Policy Statements

1. **Data Collection and Minimization**  
   1.1. The organization shall only collect PII that is necessary for legitimate business purposes and shall ensure data minimization principles are applied.

2. **Data Processing and Storage**  
   2.1. PII shall be processed and stored in a manner that ensures its confidentiality, integrity, and availability.  
   2.2. Access to PII shall be restricted to authorized personnel only, based on the principle of least privilege.

3. **Data Retention and Disposal**  
   3.1. PII shall be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or as required by law.  
   3.2. Secure disposal methods shall be employed to destroy PII that is no longer required.

4. **Data Sharing and Disclosure**  
   4.1. PII shall not be shared with third parties without explicit consent from the data subject, unless required by law or contractual obligation.  
   4.2. Data sharing agreements shall be established with third parties to ensure the protection of PII.

5. **Data Subject Rights**  
   5.1. The organization shall establish procedures to enable data subjects to exercise their rights, including access, correction, and deletion of their PII.

6. **Incident Management**  
   6.1. Any suspected or confirmed breach of PII shall be reported immediately to the [Data Privacy Officer] and managed according to the organization's incident response procedures.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Data Privacy Officer**: Responsible for overseeing the implementation of this policy, monitoring compliance, and handling data subject requests.
- **IT Department**: Ensures technical measures are in place to protect PII and supports incident response activities.
- **All Employees**: Must comply with this policy and report any security incidents or breaches involving PII.

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through regular audits and assessments. Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract. The [Data Privacy Officer] is responsible for ensuring adherence to this policy and reporting compliance status to senior management.

## Exceptions

Any exceptions to this policy must be documented and approved by the [Data Privacy Officer] and [Senior Management]. Exceptions will be granted only in circumstances where there is a valid business justification and where the risks have been assessed and mitigated.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed annually or when significant changes occur in the organization or applicable laws and regulations. The [Data Privacy Officer] is responsible for ensuring the policy remains current and effective.
