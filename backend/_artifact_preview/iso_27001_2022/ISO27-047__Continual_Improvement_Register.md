### Column Guidance

| Column | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Improvement ID | Enter a unique identifier for the improvement item, using a consistent format such as CI-YYYY-###. |
| Date Identified | Enter the date the improvement opportunity was identified in YYYY-MM-DD format. |
| Identified By | Enter the name and role/team of the person or function that identified the improvement. |
| Source of Improvement | Select or enter the origin of the opportunity, such as Internal audit, External audit, Management review, Risk assessment, Incident, Nonconformity, Corrective action, KPI/metric trend, Supplier review, Employee suggestion, Threat intelligence, Legal/regulatory change, Customer requirement, or Other. |
| Source Reference | Enter the reference to the originating record, such as audit finding ID, management review minute reference, risk ID, incident ID, corrective action ID, ticket number, or document reference. |
| ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause or Annex A Reference | Enter the relevant ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirement or control reference, such as Cl. 10.1, Cl. 9.3, Cl. 6.1, or Annex A 5.24; use N/A only if no direct clause/control applies. |
| Process / Asset / ISMS Area Affected | Enter the business process, ISMS process, system, asset group, department, site, or service affected by the improvement. |
| Improvement Opportunity Description | Clearly describe the opportunity to improve the suitability, adequacy, or effectiveness of the ISMS; do not record routine tasks unless they improve the ISMS. |
| Current Situation / Gap | Describe the current condition, limitation, inefficiency, weakness, trend, or missed opportunity that justifies the improvement. |
| Intended Improvement Outcome | Describe the measurable or observable result expected once the improvement is implemented. |
| Reason / Driver | Enter why the improvement is needed, such as reduce risk, improve control effectiveness, improve evidence quality, reduce recurrence, improve efficiency, address audit observation, meet stakeholder need, or adapt to change. |
| Risk or Benefit Statement | State the risk reduced or benefit achieved, including business or ISMS impact where known. |
| Priority | Select High, Medium, or Low based on risk reduction, regulatory/customer impact, management priority, and urgency. |
| Improvement Owner | Enter the accountable owner responsible for delivering the improvement, including name and role/team. |
| Action Plan / Tasks | List the planned actions required to deliver the improvement; include enough detail to show what will be changed, created, reviewed, automated, trained, or measured. |
| Resources Required | Enter required people, budget, tools, external support, approvals, or dependencies; enter None if no additional resources are required. |
| Target Completion Date | Enter the planned completion date in YYYY-MM-DD format. |
| Status | Select Not Started, In Progress, On Hold, Implemented - Pending Effectiveness Review, Closed - Effective, Closed - Not Effective, Cancelled, or Deferred. |
| Progress Notes | Enter concise dated updates on implementation progress, blockers, decisions, or scope changes; prefix each update with YYYY-MM-DD. |
| Effectiveness Criteria | Define how effectiveness will be verified, such as metric target, audit result, incident reduction, test result, user adoption evidence, control operating evidence, or management acceptance. |
| Effectiveness Review Date | Enter the planned or actual date for checking whether the improvement achieved its intended outcome in YYYY-MM-DD format. |
| Effectiveness Review Result | Record the outcome of the effectiveness review, such as Effective, Partially effective, Not effective, Too early to assess, or Not yet reviewed, with a short rationale. |
| Residual Actions Required | Enter any follow-up actions needed after review; enter None if no further action is required. |
| Closure Date | Enter the date the improvement was formally closed in YYYY-MM-DD format; leave blank until closure is approved. |
| Closure Approved By | Enter the name and role of the person approving closure, typically the ISMS Manager, control owner, or management review chair. |
| Evidence / Record Link | Enter links or references to supporting records, such as updated procedures, tickets, screenshots, meeting minutes, audit evidence, training records, metrics, or risk treatment updates. |

### Maintenance

The ISMS Manager owns this register, with improvement owners responsible for keeping their assigned entries current. It should be updated whenever an improvement opportunity is identified or progressed, reviewed at least monthly and at each management review, and sourced from audits, management reviews, risk assessments, incident/problem records, corrective actions, performance metrics, supplier reviews, and ISMS ticketing or workflow systems.
