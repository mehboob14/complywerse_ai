## Purpose

The purpose of this Operations Security Policy is to establish mandatory requirements for managing and securing operational processes, ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information as outlined in ISO/IEC 27001:2022, control reference A.8.x. This policy aims to mitigate risks associated with operational procedures, change management, capacity planning, malware protection, data backup, and logging.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party entities involved in the operations and management of [Organization Name]’s information systems. It covers all operational activities, including but not limited to operating procedures, change management, capacity management, malware protection, backup processes, and logging activities.

## Policy Statements

1. **Operating Procedures**
   - 1.1 All operational procedures shall be documented, maintained, and made available to all relevant personnel.
   - 1.2 Procedures shall be reviewed and updated annually or following significant changes to the environment.

2. **Change Management**
   - 2.1 Changes to information systems and processes shall be controlled and documented through a formal change management process.
   - 2.2 Emergency changes shall be authorized by [Designated Authority] and documented post-implementation.

3. **Capacity Management**
   - 3.1 Capacity requirements shall be monitored and reviewed regularly to ensure system performance and availability.
   - 3.2 Capacity planning shall be conducted annually to anticipate future needs and mitigate potential capacity issues.

4. **Malware Protection**
   - 4.1 All systems shall have up-to-date malware protection software installed and configured.
   - 4.2 Malware protection measures shall be reviewed and tested quarterly.

5. **Data Backup**
   - 5.1 Data backup procedures shall be documented and include schedules, storage locations, and restoration processes.
   - 5.2 Backups shall be tested for integrity and restoration capabilities at least twice a year.

6. **Logging and Monitoring**
   - 6.1 Logging of system activities shall be enabled for all critical systems to detect unauthorized access and anomalies.
   - 6.2 Logs shall be reviewed weekly and retained for a minimum of [Retention Period] in compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Chief Information Officer (CIO):** Overall responsibility for the implementation and enforcement of this policy.
- **IT Operations Manager:** Responsible for ensuring that operational procedures and controls are in place and followed.
- **Security Team:** Responsible for the implementation of malware protection and monitoring of security logs.
- **All Employees:** Responsible for adhering to operational procedures and reporting any security incidents.

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through regular audits and reviews conducted by the [Internal Audit Team]. Non-compliance shall be addressed through disciplinary actions as per [Organization Name]’s disciplinary procedures.

## Exceptions

Any exceptions to this policy must be formally documented and approved by the [Policy Owner/Committee]. Exceptions shall be reviewed on a case-by-case basis and must not compromise the security of information systems.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed and updated annually or in response to significant changes in the operational environment or regulatory requirements. The [Policy Owner/Committee] is responsible for ensuring the policy remains current and effective.
