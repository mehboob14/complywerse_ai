## Purpose

The purpose of this Communications Security Policy is to establish the mandatory requirements for securing network communications, ensuring network segregation, managing data transfer, and implementing web filtering in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 control references A.8.20 to A.8.23. This policy aims to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information communicated over the organization's networks.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party users who have access to the organization's information systems and networks. It covers all network communications, including internal and external data transfers, and the use of web-based resources.

## Policy Statements

1. **Network Security**  
   1.1 The organization shall implement network security controls to protect information in transit against unauthorized access, modification, or denial of service.  
   1.2 Network security controls shall include, but are not limited to, firewalls, intrusion detection/prevention systems, and secure communication protocols.

2. **Network Segregation**  
   2.1 The organization shall segregate networks based on sensitivity and criticality of information to minimize the risk of unauthorized access and data breaches.  
   2.2 Network segregation shall be achieved through the use of VLANs, subnets, and access control lists.

3. **Information Transfer**  
   3.1 The organization shall establish procedures for the secure transfer of information, ensuring that data is encrypted during transit using approved cryptographic methods.  
   3.2 All information transfers shall be logged and monitored to detect unauthorized access or anomalies.

4. **Web Filtering**  
   4.1 The organization shall implement web filtering controls to block access to unauthorized or malicious websites.  
   4.2 Web filtering policies shall be reviewed and updated regularly to address emerging threats and changes in organizational requirements.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Information Security Manager**: Responsible for overseeing the implementation and maintenance of network security controls and ensuring compliance with this policy.
- **IT Network Team**: Responsible for configuring and managing network devices, implementing network segregation, and ensuring secure data transfer protocols are in place.
- **Compliance Officer**: Responsible for monitoring adherence to this policy and reporting any non-compliance to senior management.
- **All Employees**: Responsible for adhering to the policy and reporting any security incidents or breaches immediately.

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through regular audits and network security assessments. Any violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract. The organization reserves the right to monitor network traffic and access logs to ensure compliance.

## Exceptions

Any exceptions to this policy must be documented and approved by the Information Security Manager. Requests for exceptions shall include a risk assessment and a justification for the exception.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed annually or in response to significant changes in the organization's network infrastructure or threat landscape. The Information Security Manager is responsible for ensuring this policy remains current and effective.
