## Purpose & Objectives

The purpose of this Risk Treatment Plan is to outline the selected controls and actions necessary to mitigate identified information security risks in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022, specifically addressing control reference Cl. 6.1.3 / 8.3. The objectives of this plan are to ensure that all identified risks are treated appropriately, and that the selected controls are implemented effectively to protect the organization's information assets.

## Scope

This Risk Treatment Plan applies to all information assets within the organization that have been identified as having potential risks. It encompasses the selection and implementation of controls to mitigate these risks, as well as the assignment of responsibilities and timelines for completion. The scope includes all departments and functions that manage or utilize information assets.

## Assumptions & Dependencies

- The risk assessment process has been completed and documented.
- All relevant stakeholders have been identified and are available for consultation.
- Sufficient resources are available to implement the selected controls.
- Dependencies include the availability of technical solutions and third-party service providers.

## Approach / Phases

1. **Identification of Risks**: Review the risk assessment to identify risks that require treatment.
2. **Selection of Controls**: Determine appropriate controls from Annex A of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 to mitigate identified risks.
3. **Implementation Planning**: Develop detailed action plans for the implementation of selected controls.
4. **Execution**: Implement the controls as per the action plans.
5. **Monitoring and Review**: Continuously monitor the effectiveness of controls and make necessary adjustments.
6. **Documentation and Reporting**: Maintain records of the risk treatment process and report progress to stakeholders.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Risk Management Officer**: Oversees the entire risk treatment process and ensures alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
- **Risk Analyst**: Conducts risk identification and assessment activities.
- **Security Manager**: Responsible for selecting appropriate controls and ensuring their alignment with identified risks.
- **Project Manager**: Develops and oversees the implementation plan for selected controls.
- **IT Department**: Executes the technical implementation of controls.
- **Compliance Officer**: Monitors the effectiveness of controls and ensures compliance with standards.

## Resources

- **Human Resources**: Skilled personnel from the Risk Management, IT, and Compliance departments.
- **Technical Resources**: Security tools and software required for control implementation.
- **Financial Resources**: Budget allocation for acquiring necessary tools and services.

## Success Metrics

- Reduction in the number of identified risks post-implementation.
- Percentage of controls implemented within the planned timeline.
- Improvement in audit findings related to information security.
- Stakeholder satisfaction with the risk treatment process.

## Review & Update Triggers

- Significant changes in the organizational structure or operations.
- Introduction of new information assets or technologies.
- Changes in regulatory requirements or industry standards.
- Results of internal or external audits indicating non-compliance.
