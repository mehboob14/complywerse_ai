## Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to establish a systematic approach for controlling records that demonstrate the conformity of the Information Security Management System (ISMS) with ISO/IEC 27001:2022. This ensures that records are identified, stored, protected, retrieved, retained, and disposed of in compliance with the requirements of Cl. 7.5.3.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all records generated as part of the ISMS within [Organization Name]. It covers all departments and personnel involved in the creation, management, and disposal of ISMS-related records.

## Prerequisites & Inputs

- **Prerequisites:**
  - Familiarity with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.
  - Access to the organization's document management system (DMS).

- **Inputs:**
  - ISMS policy documents.
  - Audit reports.
  - Risk assessment records.
  - Training records.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Identification of Records:**
   - Identify records necessary to demonstrate ISMS conformity and effective operation.
   - Categorize records by type, such as policies, procedures, audit logs, etc.

2. **Storage and Protection:**
   - Store records in the [Document Management System] with appropriate access controls.
   - Ensure records are protected against unauthorized access, alteration, and destruction.

3. **Retrieval:**
   - Implement a retrieval process to ensure records can be accessed efficiently by authorized personnel.
   - Document retrieval requests in the DMS log.

4. **Retention:**
   - Define retention periods for each type of record in accordance with legal, regulatory, and business requirements.
   - Review retention schedules annually for updates.

5. **Disposal:**
   - Dispose of records that have met their retention period using secure methods, such as shredding or digital wiping.
   - Document disposal actions in the DMS log.

- **Escalation:**
