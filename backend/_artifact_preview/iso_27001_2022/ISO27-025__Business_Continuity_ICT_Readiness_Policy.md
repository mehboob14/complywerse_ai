## Purpose

The purpose of this Business Continuity & ICT Readiness Policy is to establish a framework for ensuring the continuity of critical business operations and the readiness of ICT systems in the event of a disruption. This policy supports compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 controls A.5.29 and A.5.30, which require organizations to develop and implement strategies for business continuity and ICT readiness.

## Scope

This policy applies to all business units, employees, contractors, and third-party service providers involved in the operation, management, and support of [Organization Name]'s critical business functions and ICT systems. It encompasses all processes, activities, and resources necessary to maintain business continuity and ICT readiness.

## Policy Statements

1. [Organization Name] shall establish and maintain a Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) that aligns with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements to ensure the resilience of critical business operations.

2. A Business Impact Analysis (BIA) shall be conducted at least annually to identify and prioritize critical business functions and the ICT systems that support them.

3. [Organization Name] shall develop, implement, and maintain a Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and ICT Readiness Plan that address identified risks and ensure the availability of critical functions and systems.

4. The BCP and ICT Readiness Plan shall include procedures for incident response, recovery, and restoration of services to minimize disruption.

5. Regular testing and exercises of the BCP and ICT Readiness Plan shall be conducted to validate the effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

6. All employees, contractors, and third-party service providers shall be trained on their roles and responsibilities within the BCP and ICT Readiness Plan.

7. [Organization Name] shall ensure that ICT systems are designed and maintained to support business continuity requirements, including redundancy, failover capabilities, and data backup.

8. A communication strategy shall be established to ensure timely and effective communication with stakeholders during a disruption.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Business Continuity Manager**: Responsible for developing, implementing, and maintaining the BCMS, BCP, and ICT Readiness Plan.
- **ICT Manager**: Ensures that ICT systems meet business continuity requirements and coordinates ICT readiness activities.
- **Department Heads**: Identify critical business functions and resources, and support business continuity planning and exercises.
- **All Employees**: Participate in training and exercises, and adhere to the BCP and ICT Readiness Plan during disruptions.

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through regular audits and reviews of the BCMS, BCP, and ICT Readiness Plan. Non-compliance shall be addressed through corrective actions, and repeated violations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.

## Exceptions

Any exceptions to this policy must be formally documented and approved by the [Approving Authority]. Exceptions shall be reviewed periodically to assess ongoing applicability and risk.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually or upon significant changes to the organization or its operating environment. The Business Continuity Manager is responsible for ensuring the policy remains current and effective.
