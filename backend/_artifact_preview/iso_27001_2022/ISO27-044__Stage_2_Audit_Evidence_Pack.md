## What This Evidences

The **Stage 2 Audit Evidence Pack** evidences that the organisation has implemented and operated the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Internal Audit Programme in accordance with ** Internal Audit**. It demonstrates that internal audits were planned, performed, reported, and followed up before or during Stage 2 certification readiness.

This is **operational evidence** and must be **collected from the organisation’s own systems and records**. It must not be authored retrospectively as a standalone document. The pack should contain exports, records, tickets, reports, screenshots, and supporting artefacts proving that the internal audit process actually occurred.

## Where To Collect It From

Collect evidence from the systems used to manage audits, findings, corrective actions, and ISMS governance, such as:

- Audit management or GRC platform: `[GRC/audit tool name]`
- Document management system: `[SharePoint/Google Drive/Confluence/Notion/DMS]`
- ITSM or ticketing system for findings/actions: `[Jira/ServiceNow/Freshservice/Linear]`
- Risk register or compliance tool: `[risk register/GRC platform]`
- Meeting records and approvals: `[Teams/Google Workspace/Outlook/Board portal]`
- Evidence repositories used during audit: `[evidence drive/SIEM/IAM/cloud console/export folder]`

The final pack is usually assembled as a **ZIP file or controlled shared drive folder** with a clear index.

## How To Collect

1. Locate the approved internal audit programme or schedule covering the current certification period in `[GRC/audit tool/DMS]`.
2. Export the internal audit plan showing scope, criteria, audit dates, auditors, audited areas, and audit status.
3. Retrieve completed internal audit reports from `[audit tool/DMS]`, including findings, conclusions, sampled controls, and auditor sign-off.
4. Export the findings or nonconformities register from `[GRC/ITSM/ticketing tool]`.
5. For each audit finding, export the related corrective action ticket, including owner, due date, root cause, action plan, implementation evidence, and closure approval.
6. Collect evidence of management review or governance review of audit results from `[meeting records/DMS]`.
7. Capture proof of auditor independence and competence, such as audit assignment records, role descriptions, training records, or certification records from `[HR/LMS/GRC tool]`.
8. Export or save evidence in original format where possible, preserving metadata such as timestamps, owners, workflow status, and approvals.
9. Assemble all files into a structured ZIP or drive folder named `[YYYY-MM-DD]_Stage_2_Audit_Evidence_Pack`.
10. Create an index file listing each item, source system, export date, owner, and relevant ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirement.

## What To Capture

The evidence pack should include:

- Internal audit programme/schedule for the relevant audit period
- Audit scope and criteria, including ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and ISMS scope reference
- Names and roles of auditors and audited functions
- Evidence of auditor independence and competence
- Completed internal audit reports
- Audit checklists or working papers, where available
- Nonconformities, observations, and improvement opportunities
- Corrective action records linked to findings
- Root cause analysis and action tracking records
- Closure evidence and verification of effectiveness
- Management review records showing audit results were reviewed
- Timestamps, workflow status, approvals, owners, and due dates
- Export logs or screenshots showing source system and collection date

## Frequency & Owner

- **Frequency:** At least annually, and before Stage 2 certification audit; additionally after major ISMS changes or as defined in the internal audit programme.
- **Owner:** ISMS Manager or Compliance Manager.
- **Contributors:** Internal Auditor, Control Owners, Risk Owner, ITSM Administrator, HR/LMS Administrator, and Management Review Chair.

## Retention & Storage

Store the evidence pack in `[controlled evidence repository/DMS/GRC tool]` with restricted access and version control.

Retain for at least the certification cycle or the organisation’s defined retention period, whichever is longer, typically **3 years minimum**. Preserve source exports and avoid editing files after collection. If screenshots are used, include date/time, logged-in role where appropriate, and system name.

## Acceptance Criteria

An auditor will generally accept the pack as sufficient if it shows that:

- Internal audits were planned at defined intervals.
- The audit covered the ISMS scope and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.
- Audit criteria and scope were defined.
- Auditors were competent and independent from the areas audited.
- Audits were performed and results were documented.
- Findings were recorded, assigned, tracked, and closed or actively managed.
- Corrective actions include root cause analysis and evidence of implementation.
- Results were communicated to relevant management.
- Evidence is traceable to source systems, dated, complete, and not retrospectively fabricated.
- The ZIP or drive folder is organised, indexed, and readily reviewable during the Stage 2 audit.
