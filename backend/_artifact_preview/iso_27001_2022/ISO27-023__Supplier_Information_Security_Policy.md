## Purpose

The purpose of this Supplier Information Security Policy is to establish mandatory requirements for managing and protecting information shared with suppliers, ensuring compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 controls A.5.19 to A.5.22. This policy aims to safeguard the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets when engaging with suppliers.

## Scope

This policy applies to all [Organization Name] employees, contractors, and third-party suppliers who handle, process, or manage [Organization Name] information. It encompasses all types of information, including electronic, paper-based, and verbal, shared with or accessed by suppliers.

## Policy Statements

1. **Supplier Risk Assessment**  
   1.1. [Organization Name] shall conduct a risk assessment for each supplier before engagement to identify and evaluate potential information security risks.

2. **Supplier Agreements**  
   2.1. All agreements with suppliers shall include information security requirements aligned with [Organization Name]'s security policies and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 standards.  
   2.2. Agreements shall specify the responsibilities of each party concerning information security and data protection.

3. **Supplier Monitoring and Review**  
   3.1. [Organization Name] shall regularly monitor and review supplier performance against agreed information security requirements.  
   3.2. Suppliers shall be subject to periodic audits to ensure compliance with contractual obligations and information security standards.

4. **Supplier Termination**  
   4.1. Upon termination of a supplier relationship, [Organization Name] shall ensure that all organizational information is returned or securely destroyed.  
   4.2. Access rights to [Organization Name] systems and information shall be revoked immediately upon contract termination.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Supplier Relationship Manager**: Responsible for conducting supplier risk assessments and ensuring compliance with this policy.
- **Information Security Officer**: Oversees the implementation of information security requirements in supplier agreements and conducts periodic audits.
- **Legal Department**: Ensures that all supplier agreements include appropriate information security clauses.
- **IT Department**: Manages access controls and ensures secure data transfer between [Organization Name] and suppliers.

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy is mandatory. The Information Security Officer shall monitor adherence to this policy and report any non-compliance to senior management. Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.

## Exceptions

Any exceptions to this policy must be documented and approved by the Information Security Officer and the Legal Department. Exceptions shall be granted on a case-by-case basis and must not compromise the security of [Organization Name]'s information.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed annually or upon significant changes to the organization's supplier relationships or information security landscape. The Information Security Officer is responsible for ensuring the policy remains current and effective. Any updates shall be communicated to all relevant stakeholders.
