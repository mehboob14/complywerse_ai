## Purpose

The purpose of this Physical & Environmental Security Policy is to establish and maintain secure physical and environmental conditions for all organizational sites and assets. This policy aims to protect information assets from unauthorized physical access, damage, and interference, in alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 control A.7.x requirements.

## Scope

This policy applies to all physical locations owned, leased, or operated by [Organization Name], including data centers, offices, and remote facilities. It covers all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers who access these locations.

## Policy Statements

1. **Physical Security Perimeter**: All facilities containing sensitive information shall have defined physical security perimeters that prevent unauthorized physical access, damage, and interference.
   
2. **Secure Areas**: Secure areas shall be protected by appropriate entry controls to ensure that only authorized personnel have access. Entry controls shall include, but are not limited to, ID badges, access cards, and biometric systems.

3. **Equipment Security**: All critical equipment shall be located in secure areas to prevent unauthorized access, damage, and interference. Equipment shall be protected from environmental hazards and opportunities for theft.

4. **Environmental Controls**: Facilities shall be equipped with adequate environmental controls, including fire suppression systems, temperature and humidity controls, and power supply protection to safeguard equipment and information.

5. **Visitor Management**: All visitors to secure areas shall be logged and escorted by authorized personnel. Visitor logs shall be maintained for a minimum of [X months/years].

6. **Physical Security Monitoring**: Surveillance systems shall be deployed to monitor secure areas continuously. Recorded footage shall be retained for a minimum of [X days/months] for review and investigation purposes.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Information Security Manager**: Responsible for the implementation and oversight of this policy, ensuring compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 standards.
- **Facilities Manager**: Responsible for maintaining physical security measures and environmental controls in all organizational facilities.
- **IT Department**: Responsible for ensuring that all equipment is properly secured and protected from environmental risks.
- **All Employees**: Required to comply with this policy and report any security incidents or breaches to the Information Security Manager immediately.

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through regular audits and inspections. Non-compliance shall be addressed through corrective actions, which may include disciplinary measures up to and including termination of employment for serious breaches.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be documented and approved by the Information Security Manager. All exceptions shall be reviewed annually to determine if they remain valid.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually or when significant changes occur in the organization or its operating environment. The Information Security Manager is responsible for ensuring the policy remains current and effective.
