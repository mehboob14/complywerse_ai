## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to define the scope of the Information Security Management System (ISMS) in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Clause 4.3. This document establishes the boundaries and applicability of the ISMS within [Organization Name], ensuring that all relevant locations, business units, services, assets, and networks are identified and managed effectively.

## Scope

This policy applies to all departments, employees, contractors, and third-party partners involved in the operation and management of information security within [Organization Name]. The ISMS scope includes:

- All physical locations owned or operated by [Organization Name], including [Headquarters, Regional Offices, Data Centers].
- Business units providing [specific services] and supporting functions.
- Information assets including hardware, software, and data that support business operations.
- Networks and communication systems used to transmit, store, and process information.

## Policy Statements

1. The ISMS shall encompass all information assets, processes, and systems that support the delivery of [Organization Name]'s services and operations.
2. All physical locations where information is processed, stored, or transmitted shall be included within the ISMS scope.
3. The ISMS scope shall be reviewed and updated annually or upon significant changes to the organization’s structure, technology, or business processes.
4. The ISMS shall be aligned with the strategic objectives of [Organization Name] and support compliance with applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.
5. The ISMS scope shall be documented and communicated to all relevant stakeholders to ensure clarity and understanding.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **ISMS Manager**: Responsible for defining, maintaining, and reviewing the ISMS scope in alignment with organizational objectives and regulatory requirements.
- **Department Heads**: Ensure that their respective areas comply with the ISMS scope and report any changes that may impact the scope.
- **IT Security Team**: Support the ISMS Manager in identifying and managing information assets within the defined scope.
- **All Employees**: Adhere to the ISMS policies and report any discrepancies or changes in their work areas that might affect the ISMS scope.

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through regular audits and reviews conducted by the ISMS Manager. Non-compliance shall be addressed through corrective actions, which may include training, disciplinary measures, or process improvements. The ISMS Manager shall report compliance status to senior management on a quarterly basis.

## Exceptions

Any exceptions to this policy must be formally documented and approved by the ISMS Manager. Exceptions shall be reviewed regularly to ensure they remain valid and do not compromise the security objectives of the organization.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually or when significant changes occur in the organization’s structure or operations. The ISMS Manager is responsible for ensuring the policy remains relevant and effective, incorporating feedback from audits, incidents, and changes in the external environment.
