## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish, implement, and maintain information security objectives that align with the strategic direction of [Organization Name] and comply with the requirements of ISO/IEC 27001:2022, specifically clause 6.2. This policy ensures that information security objectives are measurable, consistent with the information security policy, and take into account applicable information security requirements.

## Scope

This policy applies to all departments, employees, and contractors within [Organization Name] who are involved in the development, implementation, and maintenance of information security objectives. It covers all information assets, processes, and systems that are part of the organization's information security management system (ISMS).

## Policy Statements

1. Information security objectives shall be established at relevant functions and levels within the organization, and shall be consistent with the information security policy and the strategic direction of [Organization Name].
   
2. Information security objectives shall be measurable and shall include criteria for evaluating performance against these objectives.

3. Objectives shall be documented and communicated to all relevant stakeholders to ensure awareness and understanding.

4. Information security objectives shall be reviewed and updated at planned intervals or when significant changes occur to ensure continued relevance and alignment with the organization's risk assessment and risk treatment plan.

5. The achievement of information security objectives shall be monitored and reported to the management at regular intervals.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Information Security Manager**: Responsible for coordinating the development, implementation, and monitoring of information security objectives. Ensures alignment with the organization's strategic goals and compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
  
- **Department Heads**: Responsible for establishing and monitoring information security objectives within their respective departments, ensuring they align with organizational objectives.

- **All Employees**: Responsible for understanding and contributing to the achievement of information security objectives relevant to their roles.

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy will be monitored through regular audits and reviews of the information security objectives. Non-compliance shall be addressed through corrective actions, which may include additional training, process improvements, or disciplinary actions as necessary.

## Exceptions

Any exceptions to this policy must be formally documented and approved by the Information Security Manager. Exceptions will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis and must include a risk assessment and mitigation plan.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed annually or when significant changes occur in the organization or its environment. The Information Security Manager is responsible for ensuring that this policy remains relevant and effective in achieving the desired information security objectives. Any updates or changes to this policy shall be communicated to all relevant stakeholders.
