## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to maintain a comprehensive record of competence and training activities within the organization, as mandated by ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Clause 7.2 and Control A.6.3. This record ensures that all personnel involved in the Information Security Management System (ISMS) are adequately trained and competent to fulfill their roles.

## Scope

This record applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party personnel who are involved in the ISMS processes and activities. It covers all training sessions conducted, including internal, external, and on-the-job training relevant to information security.

## Captured Fields

| Field             | Description                                                                 |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Training ID       | Unique identifier for each training session.                                |
| Training Title    | The title or name of the training program.                                  |
| Date Conducted    | The date on which the training was conducted.                               |
| Trainer Name      | The name of the person or organization delivering the training.             |
| Attendee Name     | The name of the individual attending the training.                          |
| Department        | The department or team to which the attendee belongs.                       |
| Training Type     | The nature of the training (e.g., Internal, External, On-the-job).          |
| Duration (hours)  | The length of the training session in hours.                                |
| Score/Outcome     | The result or score achieved by the attendee, if applicable.                |
| Competence Level  | The level of competence achieved post-training (e.g., Basic, Intermediate). |
| Certification     | Any certification received upon completion of the training, if applicable.  |
| Comments          | Additional remarks or observations about the training.                      |
