## Purpose

The purpose of this Acceptable Use Policy is to establish the rules for the use of information, IT assets, and processing facilities within [Organization Name]. This policy aims to protect the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of information by ensuring that all users understand and comply with the acceptable use of these resources.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, temporary staff, and other workers at [Organization Name], including all personnel affiliated with third parties. It covers all information assets, IT systems, and processing facilities owned or leased by [Organization Name].

## Policy Statements

1. Users shall use information assets, IT systems, and processing facilities only for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with [Organization Name]'s policies and applicable laws.
2. Users shall not use [Organization Name]'s resources to engage in any activity that is illegal, offensive, or disruptive to the business operations.
3. Users shall ensure that all access to information and systems is authorized and that credentials are kept confidential and secure.
4. Users shall not install unauthorized software or hardware on [Organization Name]'s systems or networks.
5. Users shall report any security incidents, breaches, or suspicious activities immediately to the [IT Security Team].
6. Users shall ensure that all data is handled in compliance with [Organization Name]'s data protection policies and relevant legal requirements.
7. Users shall not use [Organization Name]'s resources to store or transmit personal or sensitive information without appropriate authorization and encryption.
8. Users shall adhere to the guidelines for secure communication, including the use of email, internet, and social media, as defined by [Organization Name].
9. Users shall not attempt to bypass security controls or exploit vulnerabilities in [Organization Name]'s systems.
10. Users shall participate in mandatory security awareness training as required by [Organization Name].

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **IT Security Lead**: Responsible for the implementation and enforcement of this policy.
- **Department Heads**: Ensure their teams are aware of and comply with this policy.
- **IT Support Team**: Provide technical support and guidance on the use of IT systems.
- **All Users**: Comply with this policy and report any violations to the appropriate authority.

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy is mandatory. [Organization Name] will monitor the use of its information assets and IT systems to ensure adherence to this policy. Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract. Regular audits will be conducted to assess compliance and identify areas for improvement.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted only by the [IT Security Lead] or an authorized delegate. All exceptions must be documented, including the rationale and duration, and reviewed periodically.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed annually or as required by changes in legislation, regulations, or organizational requirements. The [IT Security Lead] is responsible for ensuring the policy remains relevant and up-to-date. Feedback from users and stakeholders will be considered during the review process.
