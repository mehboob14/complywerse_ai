## Purpose

The purpose of this Information Security Policy is to establish a framework for managing information security within [Organization Name] in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022. This policy demonstrates our commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets and supporting our strategic business objectives.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers who have access to [Organization Name]'s information assets. It covers all information systems, networks, and data owned or managed by [Organization Name].

## Policy Statements

1. [Organization Name] shall establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve an Information Security Management System (ISMS) in accordance with the requirements of ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
2. Information security objectives shall be defined, documented, and aligned with the strategic goals of [Organization Name]. These objectives shall be measurable and reviewed regularly.
3. All information assets shall be identified, classified, and protected in accordance with their value, legal requirements, sensitivity, and criticality to the organization.
4. Risk assessments shall be conducted regularly to identify potential threats and vulnerabilities to information assets. Appropriate risk treatment plans shall be developed and implemented.
5. All employees and contractors shall receive appropriate information security training and awareness to ensure compliance with this policy and related procedures.
6. Security incidents shall be reported and managed in accordance with the established incident management procedures to minimize impact and prevent recurrence.
7. Compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations related to information security shall be ensured and documented.

## Roles & Responsibilities

| Role                         | Responsibility                                                                 |
|------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) | Oversee the implementation and maintenance of the ISMS. Ensure compliance with this policy. |
| Information Security Manager | Conduct risk assessments and develop risk treatment plans. Provide training and awareness programs. |
| IT Department                | Implement technical controls and monitor information systems for security incidents. |
| All Employees                | Comply with information security policies and report any security incidents.   |

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through regular audits and assessments. Non-compliance shall be addressed through corrective actions and may result in disciplinary measures in accordance with [Organization Name]'s disciplinary procedures. 

## Exceptions

Any exceptions to this policy must be formally documented and approved by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). Exceptions shall be reviewed periodically to determine if they remain valid.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually or when significant changes occur to ensure its continued relevance and effectiveness. The Information Security Manager is responsible for coordinating the review process and updating the policy as necessary.
