## Maintenance Process & Cadence

The Asset Inventory / Register is maintained through a continuous process of asset identification, classification, and review. Updates to the register are made as new assets are acquired, existing assets are decommissioned, or asset details change. A formal review of the register is conducted semi-annually to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Asset Owners**: Responsible for the accurate classification and regular review of their assigned assets.
- **IT Department**: Facilitates the maintenance of the register, ensuring technical details are up-to-date.
- **Information Security Officer**: Oversees the compliance of the register with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 standards and coordinates the semi-annual review process.

## Review

This document and the Asset Inventory / Register it describes will be reviewed semi-annually or as significant changes occur in the organization's asset management practices.
