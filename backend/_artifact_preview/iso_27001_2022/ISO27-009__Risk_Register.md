## Purpose

The purpose of this Risk Register is to systematically document and manage information security risks in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022. It facilitates the identification, assessment, and treatment of risks, ensuring that residual risks are within acceptable limits and aligned with the organization's risk appetite.

## Scope

This Risk Register applies to all information security risks identified within the scope of the organization's Information Security Management System (ISMS), as defined by ISO/IEC 27001:2022. It includes risks associated with information assets, business processes, and IT systems.

## Maintenance Process & Cadence

The Risk Register is maintained through regular updates and reviews. Risk assessments are conducted annually or when significant changes occur in the organization or its environment. New risks are added as they are identified, and existing entries are updated to reflect changes in risk status, treatment plans, or residual risk levels. The Risk Register is reviewed quarterly by the Information Security Management Team to ensure its accuracy and relevance.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Risk Owner**: Responsible for managing assigned risks, implementing treatment plans, and reporting status updates.
- **Information Security Manager**: Oversees the maintenance of the Risk Register, ensures compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022, and facilitates risk assessment activities.
- **ISMS Steering Committee**: Provides oversight and approves risk treatment plans and residual risk acceptance.

## Review

The Risk Register is subject to an annual review as part of the organization's internal audit process. The review ensures that the register remains aligned with the organization's risk management objectives and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements. Changes to the Risk Register are documented, and updates are communicated to all relevant stakeholders.
