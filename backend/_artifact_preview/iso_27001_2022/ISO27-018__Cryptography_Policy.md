## Purpose

The purpose of this Cryptography Policy is to ensure the proper and effective use of cryptography to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information within [Organization Name]. This policy supports compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022, specifically control A.8.24, by establishing requirements for cryptographic controls and key management.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers who manage or use cryptographic controls on behalf of [Organization Name]. It covers all information systems, applications, and data that require cryptographic protection.

## Policy Statements

1. Cryptographic controls shall be implemented to protect information based on its classification and risk assessment results.
2. All cryptographic algorithms and key lengths used shall meet or exceed the current industry standards and regulatory requirements.
3. Cryptographic keys shall be generated, distributed, stored, and retired securely to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure.
4. Key management processes shall be documented and include key generation, distribution, storage, rotation, and destruction.
5. Access to cryptographic keys shall be restricted to authorized personnel only, based on the principle of least privilege.
6. Cryptographic controls shall be reviewed and updated regularly to address emerging threats and vulnerabilities.
7. All cryptographic incidents shall be reported and managed in accordance with the Incident Management Policy.

## Roles & Responsibilities

| Role                  | Responsibility                                                                 |
|-----------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) | Ensure overall compliance with the Cryptography Policy.                   |
| IT Security Manager   | Implement and maintain cryptographic controls and key management processes.    |
| System Administrators | Manage cryptographic keys and ensure secure configurations of systems.         |
| Employees & Contractors | Adhere to cryptographic policies and report any incidents or anomalies.        |

## Compliance, Monitoring & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through regular audits and reviews conducted by the IT Security team. Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract. Automated tools shall be used where feasible to monitor the use of cryptographic controls.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted only by the CISO and must be documented, including the rationale and any compensating controls implemented. All exceptions shall be reviewed annually to determine if they remain valid.

## Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed and updated at least annually or when significant changes occur in the organization or its environment. The CISO is responsible for ensuring the policy remains relevant and effective.
