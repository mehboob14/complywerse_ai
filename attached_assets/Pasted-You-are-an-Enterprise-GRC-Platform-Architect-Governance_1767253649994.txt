You are an Enterprise GRC Platform Architect, Governance Risk and Compliance Subject Matter Expert, and Senior Full Stack Engineer.

Your task is to design and guide the implementation of an enterprise scale Governance Risk and Compliance platform that meets regulatory expectations, supports institutional risk management, and scales across organizations, jurisdictions, and regulatory regimes.

Foundational Architecture Requirements

Design the platform with Multi Tenancy enforced at the core architecture level. All data, workflows, and permissions must be strictly isolated per tenant while allowing centralized administrative oversight where explicitly permitted.

Implement role based access control across all modules with granular permissions. Access control must be supported at tenant level, business unit level, and functional level. Each role must support full access, limited access, or read only access depending on assigned responsibilities.

Every module must be permission aware and must generate a complete audit trail for all actions.

Compliance and Regulatory Framework Coverage

Implement certification aligned standards and regulatory frameworks as first class compliance programs, including:

PCI DSS
ISO 27001
ISO 20000
NIST Cybersecurity Framework
SWIFT Customer Security Framework
Central Bank of Bahrain Technology and Cybersecurity Regulatory Requirements
SAMA Technical and Financial Regulatory Requirements
State Bank of Pakistan Enterprise Technology Governance and Risk Management Framework

Certification based standards and regulatory frameworks must coexist within a single compliance engine while respecting differences in enforcement, audit expectations, and lifecycle behavior.

Mandatory Framework Normalization Model

Each framework must be normalized into the same structure used for ISO and PCI.

1. Framework Metadata

Each framework must include:

Framework name
Regulator or authority
Jurisdiction
Mandatory versus recommended controls
Enforcement and supervisory expectations

2. Domains and Control Families

Every framework must be normalized into explicit and enforceable structures, including:

Domains
Control objectives
Controls
Sub controls

If a framework is principle based or category based, it must be converted into explicit control statements that can be assessed, evidenced, tested, and audited.

3. Standardized Control Objects

Each control must be modeled as a standardized control object containing:

Control statement
Control objective
Control owner
AI driven required evidences
Implementation guidance
Testing and assessment guidance

Each control must support deterministic mapping to:

ISO 27001
PCI DSS
NIST Cybersecurity Framework
ISO 20000
SWIFT Customer Security Framework
Central Bank of Bahrain Technology and Cybersecurity Regulatory Requirements
SAMA Technical and Financial Regulatory Requirements
State Bank of Pakistan Enterprise Technology Governance and Risk Management Framework
Any custom institutional or regulatory compliance requirement

Normalized Control Model and Control Reuse

Implement a normalized control model that acts as the single source of truth for control implementation.

Multiple framework specific controls must be mapped to a single normalized control. Each normalized control must maintain:

A unified control definition
Mapped framework controls across all standards and regulators
Required evidences
Testing and assessment procedures
Assessment results and maturity indicators

Control reuse must be enforced to eliminate duplication and to allow maturity uplift across all mapped frameworks.

Evidence Management and AI Driven Evidence Assessment

Implement a centralized Evidence Management module applicable across the entire platform.

The module must allow users to upload, manage, version, and review evidences in multiple formats.

AI driven evidence assessment must be implemented to:

Analyze uploaded evidence content
Determine relevance and adequacy against control requirements
Map a single evidence artifact to one or multiple controls across different frameworks
Map evidence to compliance requirements irrespective of the originating standard or framework
Identify gaps, weaknesses, or missing evidence
Provide confidence scoring and audit readiness indicators

Evidence must be reusable across frameworks and controls without duplication.

All evidence activities must be auditable and traceable to users, controls, frameworks, risks, and review outcomes.

Enterprise Risk Management Module

Design and implement a comprehensive Risk Management module that operates independently of individual frameworks while integrating with all of them.

The module must support enterprise wide institutional risks including strategic, operational, financial, compliance, technology, and third party risks.

Each risk must support inherent risk scoring, residual risk scoring, risk appetite alignment, treatment plans, ownership assignment, and continuous monitoring.

Risks must be linkable to normalized controls, framework controls, IT assets, evidences, issues, policies, and governance objectives.

Governance Module as the Central Control Layer

Build a Governance module that acts as the central orchestration and accountability layer across the platform.

The Governance module must establish linkages between organizational objectives, policies, standards, procedures, controls, risks, issues, assets, evidences, and compliance programs.

Governance must enable management oversight, exception handling, accountability tracking, and defensible decision making.

Policy and Document Management

Implement complete organizational document management capabilities including:

Policy and procedure authoring
Version history and traceability
Approval workflows
Periodic review cycles
Ownership assignment
Lifecycle state management
Audit ready traceability

All documents must be linkable to controls, risks, IT assets, evidences, and regulatory obligations.

IT Asset Inventory and Valuation Module

Implement an IT Asset Inventory module that supports enterprise wide asset management.

The module must allow users to:

Register and classify IT assets including applications, infrastructure, data assets, cloud resources, and third party systems
Assign ownership, business criticality, confidentiality, integrity, and availability ratings
Perform IT asset valuation based on financial impact, operational dependency, and regulatory exposure
Link IT assets to normalized controls, framework controls, risks, evidences, and compliance requirements
Support asset level risk assessment and control coverage analysis

IT assets must be reusable across all standards, frameworks, and regulatory requirements.

Compliance Initiation and Custom Framework Support

Enable users to initiate compliance programs for any supported certification or regulatory framework.

Provide the ability to upload and manage custom organizational or regulatory frameworks, guidelines, and standards. Custom frameworks must follow the same normalization model, control mapping rules, evidence workflows, and reporting structure as built in frameworks.

Technical Architecture Requirements

The backend must be implemented in Python and integrated into an already running server.

All new backend functionality must be implemented using clearly separated routers to preserve modularity, extensibility, and long term maintainability.

The frontend must be implemented using Next js with Tailwind CSS and written strictly in TypeScript.

All backend communication must be centralized through a configurable base URL stored in environment variables using a .env file.

Platform Quality Expectations

This platform is not a checklist or document repository system.

It must support regulator scrutiny, executive governance, institutional risk coverage, AI assisted evidence validation, continuous compliance monitoring, and long term scalability across multiple jurisdictions and regulators.

Begin by designing the core Multi Tenancy and access control architecture, then implement framework normalization, normalized controls, evidence management, IT asset inventory, enterprise risk management, governance orchestration, and reporting and oversight capabilities.
