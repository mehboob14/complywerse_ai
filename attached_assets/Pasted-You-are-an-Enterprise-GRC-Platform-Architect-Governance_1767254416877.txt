You are an Enterprise GRC Platform Architect, Governance Risk and Compliance Subject Matter Expert, and Senior Full Stack Engineer.

You are tasked with designing and generating an enterprise-scale Governance Risk and Compliance platform with the following requirements:

1. Technical Architecture Requirements

Backend: Python, integrated into an existing server.

Modularity: All backend functionality must be implemented as separate routers for each domain (frameworks, controls, evidence, risks, governance, documents, assets).

Frontend: Next.js + Tailwind CSS, written in TypeScript.

API Communication: Centralized through a configurable base URL stored in a .env file.

Audit & Security: All actions must be logged and auditable; system must enforce tenant isolation and role-based access control at tenant, business unit, and functional levels.

2. Compliance & Regulatory Frameworks

Implement certification and regulatory frameworks including:
PCI DSS, ISO 27001, ISO 20000, NIST CSF, SWIFT Customer Security Framework, Central Bank of Bahrain Technology & Cybersecurity Requirements, SAMA Technical & Financial Regulatory Requirements, State Bank of Pakistan Governance & Risk Framework, and any custom institutional frameworks.

3. Framework Normalization

Each framework must be normalized into a standardized structure:

Framework Metadata:

Name, Regulator / Authority, Jurisdiction

Mandatory vs Recommended Controls

Enforcement Expectations

Domains / Control Families:

Domains, Control Objectives, Controls, Sub-Controls

Convert principle-based or category-based frameworks into explicit, auditable controls

Standardized Control Objects:

Control Statement, Control Objective, Control Owner

AI-driven Required Evidences

Implementation Guidance, Testing Guidance

Mapping to ISO 27001, PCI DSS, NIST CSF, ISO 20000, SWIFT CSP, CBB, SAMA, SBP, and custom frameworks

4. Normalized Control Model

Create a single source of truth for controls

Map multiple framework-specific controls to a single normalized control

Include required evidences, testing procedures, assessment results, and maturity indicators

Enable cross-framework control reuse

5. Evidence Management with AI

Users can upload, manage, and version evidences

AI assesses evidence quality and relevance

Evidence can be mapped to single or multiple controls across frameworks

Provide confidence scoring, gap detection, and audit readiness indicators

Reusable evidence across frameworks and controls

6. IT Asset Inventory & Valuation

Register and classify IT assets (applications, infrastructure, data, cloud resources, third-party systems)

Assign ownership, criticality, confidentiality, integrity, availability, and valuation

Link IT assets to normalized controls, framework controls, risks, evidences, and compliance requirements

Support asset-level risk assessment and coverage analysis

7. Enterprise Risk Management

Support strategic, operational, financial, compliance, technology, and third-party risks

Inherent and residual risk scoring, treatment plans, and continuous monitoring

Link risks to normalized controls, framework controls, IT assets, evidences, and governance objects

8. Governance Module

Central orchestration of policies, procedures, controls, risks, issues, IT assets, evidences, and compliance programs

Support management oversight, exception handling, accountability tracking, and audit traceability

9. Policy & Document Management

Policy/procedure authoring, versioning, approvals, lifecycle management

Link documents to controls, risks, IT assets, and evidences

Enable periodic review cycles and audit-ready traceability

10. Compliance Initiation & Custom Framework Support

Users can initiate compliance programs for any supported framework

Upload and manage custom frameworks with same normalization, mapping, evidence workflows, and reporting

11. Reporting & Dashboards

Framework-level compliance posture, control coverage, evidence mapping, high-risk gaps

Enterprise-wide risk dashboards and maturity metrics

Executive/board-ready dashboards with audit traceability

Instructions for Replit AI

Generate a full modular backend structure: routers for frameworks, controls, evidence, risks, governance, documents, and assets

Generate Next.js + Tailwind + TypeScript frontend components for each module, including pages, dashboards, and forms

Implement a centralized API service layer consuming the base URL from .env

Include stubs for AI-driven evidence assessment with hooks for frontend display

Include example normalized framework, controls, risks, evidence, and IT assets to illustrate data structure

The output should be a complete platform skeleton, not a simple task list
