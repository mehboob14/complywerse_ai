Build a complete Governance Module for an enterprise-grade GRC platform.
The module must support direction, accountability, oversight, and regulatory governance across the organization.

1. Core Purpose

The Governance module must:

Define how the organization is directed and controlled

Establish accountability and ownership

Enable management and board-level oversight

Support regulatory and internal governance requirements

Integrate tightly with Risk, Compliance, Controls, Assets, and Audit modules

2. Governance Content Management

Provide full lifecycle management for governance artifacts:

Policies

Standards

Procedures

Guidelines

Charters

Framework documents

Each document must support:

Version control

Approval workflows

Ownership and reviewers

Review and expiry dates

Mapping to risks, controls, and regulatory requirements

Evidence attachment

3. Governance Structure

Implement organizational governance structures including:

Board of Directors

Executive Management

Committees (Risk, Audit, IT, Compliance, etc.)

Roles and responsibilities

RACI matrix (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed)

Capabilities:

Assign users to governance roles

Link roles to controls, risks, and approvals

Multi-tenant and department-level governance support

4. Risk Appetite & Tolerance

Enable definition and management of:

Enterprise risk appetite statements

Risk tolerance thresholds (by risk category)

Qualitative and quantitative limits

Integrations:

Link risk appetite to risk scoring logic

Trigger alerts when tolerance thresholds are breached

Use risk appetite in risk acceptance and escalation workflows

5. Delegation of Authority (DoA)

Implement a Delegation of Authority framework:

Define approval limits (financial, operational, risk acceptance)

Assign authority levels by role or position

Enforce approvals across Risk, Compliance, Audit, and Exception workflows

6. Exception & Waiver Management

Support controlled deviation from policies and controls:

Exception requests with justification

Risk assessment for each exception

Approval workflows

Expiry dates and review reminders

Link exceptions to risks, controls, and evidences

7. Management Reviews

Enable structured governance reviews:

Management review meetings

Board and committee reviews

Review inputs (KPIs, risks, incidents, audits)

Review outputs (decisions, actions, follow-ups)

Features:

Meeting agendas and minutes

Action tracking

Evidence upload

8. Regulatory Obligations Register

Maintain a centralized regulatory register:

Applicable laws, regulations, circulars, and directives

Mapping to internal policies, controls, and risks

Ownership and compliance status tracking

Applicability by jurisdiction, regulator, and business unit

9. Strategic Objectives & KPIs

Support governance-level performance management:

Define strategic objectives

Link objectives to risks, controls, and KPIs

Track governance and compliance KPIs

Dashboard views for executives and board members

10. Integration & Traceability

Ensure full traceability with:

Risk Management Module

Unified Control Library

Compliance & Certification Modules

Asset Management (IT & Organizational Assets)

Evidence Repository

Audit & Findings Management

11. Access Control & Auditability

Role-based access control

Full audit trail for governance decisions

Change history for policies and governance artifacts

12. UI / UX Expectations

Executive-friendly dashboards

Clear governance hierarchy views

Status indicators (Approved, Under Review, Expired, Exception Applied)

Search and filtering across governance artifacts

13. Non-Functional Requirements

Multi-tenant architecture

Scalable for multiple frameworks and regulators

API-ready for integrations

Secure, auditable, and compliant by design
