# 🔄 PCI DSS Lifecycle — Super Detailed Workflow

---

## **Phase 1 — Scope & Controls Definition**
**Role: Business Owner**
- Defines PCI scope (Cardholder Data Environment).
- Approves which systems, processes, and data fall under PCI DSS.
- Confirms business objectives for compliance.

**System Actions**
- Loads seeded **Controls** (Firewall, Encryption, Logging, Antivirus, Access Control).
- Maps each control to PCI DSS requirement (e.g., Req 1.1 Firewall).
- Displays **Controls & Evidence tab**:
  - Control name, PCI requirement, description.
  - Required evidence items (policy doc, config snapshot, log sample).
  - Status column (initially “Not Started”).

**Output**
- Business Owner sees the full inventory of controls and required evidence.
- IT team knows what evidence is expected.

---

## **Phase 2 — Evidence Collection & Gap Assessment**
**Role: IT/Security**
- Uploads evidence files (policies, configs, logs).
- Uses Upload Evidence page (FormData → backend saves file in `/uploads/`).

**System Actions**
- Compares **RequiredEvidence vs UploadedEvidence** for each control.
- Calculates control status:
  - **Not Started** → no evidence uploaded.
  - **Partial** → some evidence uploaded.
  - **Complete** → all required evidence uploaded.
- Evidence Checklist page:
  - Left column = Required evidence items.
  - Right column = Uploaded evidence items.
  - Missing items highlighted in red.
  - Inline “Upload Missing Evidence” button.

**Output**
- IT sees gaps clearly.
- Controls move from “Not Started” → “Partial” → “Complete” as evidence is uploaded.

---

## **Phase 3 — Audit/QSA Validation**
**Role: Audit/QSA**
- Reviews uploaded evidence.
- Marks evidence as **Accepted** or **Rejected**.

**System Actions**
- Endpoint: PATCH `/api/evidence/{id}/review`.
- If **Accepted** → evidence validated.
- If **Rejected** → auto‑create **Finding**:
  - Finding linked to control.
  - Status = Open.
  - Description = reason for rejection.
- Audit Dashboard:
  - Shows controls + evidence with Accept/Reject buttons.
- Findings Dashboard:
  - Lists all gaps with remediation tasks.

**Output**
- Audit/QSA validates compliance.
- Findings created automatically for rejected evidence.

---

## **Phase 4 — Remediation**
**Role: IT/Security**
- Reviews Findings dashboard.
- Uploads corrected evidence linked to Finding.

**System Actions**
- Updates Finding status:
  - Open → Closed when evidence accepted.
- IT Dashboard:
  - Shows Findings list.
  - Closed findings marked with green check.

**Output**
- Gaps closed.
- Audit/QSA confirms remediation loop is complete.

---

## **Phase 5 — Risk Register**
**Role: Business Owner**
- Reviews residual risks (e.g., incomplete evidence, accepted exceptions).
- Approves or rejects risk acceptance.

**System Actions**
- RiskRegister table tracks:
  - Risk description.
  - Owner.
  - Status (Accepted, Pending).
  - Approval history (timestamps, approver).
- Risk Register dashboard:
  - Approve/Reject buttons.
  - Metrics: % risks accepted vs pending.

**Output**
- Business Owner formally accepts or rejects risks.
- Risk Register updated with history.

---

## **Phase 6 — Reporting**
**Role: Audit/QSA**
- Generates compliance reports.

**System Actions**
- Endpoints: `/api/reports/roc`, `/api/reports/aoc`.
- Render HTML templates with compliance summary:
  - % controls validated.
  - % risks accepted.
  - Overall compliance status.
- Convert to PDF (WeasyPrint/pdfkit).
- Compliance Report page:
  - “Download ROC/AOC” button.
  - Dashboard metrics.

**Output**
- ROC (Report on Compliance) and AOC (Attestation of Compliance) available as PDF/HTML.
- Business Owner and Audit/QSA can share reports with stakeholders.

---

## **Phase 7 — Continuous Monitoring**
**Role: System + IT + Audit**
- System runs scheduled jobs (quarterly scans).
- IT uploads updated evidence when required.
- Audit validates monitoring results.

**System Actions**
- APScheduler job simulates quarterly scans.
- Endpoint: `/api/monitoring/run` for manual trigger.
- Auto‑creates Findings if evidence is stale (e.g., older than 90 days).
- Monitoring Dashboard:
  - Shows next scheduled check.
  - Displays monitoring results.

**Output**
- Compliance is continuously monitored.
- Stale evidence flagged automatically.
- IT and Audit keep compliance current.

---

## **Phase 8 — Roles & Authentication**
**Role: All Users**
- IT, Audit, Business Owner log in with seeded accounts.

**System Actions**
- JWT login endpoint.
- Role enforcement:
  - IT → upload/remediate evidence.
  - Audit → review evidence, issue findings.
  - Business Owner → approve risks, compliance status.
- Frontend hides unauthorized actions per role.

**Output**
- Secure, role‑specific workflows.
- Users only see actions allowed by their role.

---

# 🧠 In Simple Words
- **Start:** Business Owner defines scope and controls.  
- **Middle:** IT uploads evidence → Audit validates → Findings loop → Remediation.  
- **End:** Business Owner approves risks → Audit issues ROC/AOC.  
- **Ongoing:** Continuous monitoring keeps compliance alive.
